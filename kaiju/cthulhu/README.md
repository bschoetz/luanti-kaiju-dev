# Cthulhu

A giant amphibious sea monster for [Mineclonia](https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia),
about 13 nodes tall. He sleeps on the sea floor in the pose of his idol and is neutral
until someone enters his territory: then he rises, roars, and fights whoever it is, in the
water and for a short while on land. He grabs players and small mobs and holds them under
water, clouds the minds of players near him, heals in the sea, and dives away when he is
badly hurt.

This document is for developers who want to change or extend the mob. It follows the
READMEs of the other kaiju ([`../gojira/README.md`](../gojira/README.md),
[`../ghidorah/README.md`](../ghidorah/README.md)) and links to
[`../kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) for the shared mechanics.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `kaiju/cthulhu/init.lua` | Mob definition, sea mode, territory, grab, aura |
| `kaiju/cthulhu/mod.conf` | Mod metadata and dependencies (`mcl_mobs`, `mcl_util`, `mcl_potions`, `mobs_mc`, `kaiju_core`) |
| `kaiju/cthulhu/models/cthulhu.glb` | Generated by `tools/cthulhu_model.py` (do not edit by hand) |
| `kaiju/cthulhu/textures/cthulhu.png` | Generated by `tools/cthulhu_texture.py` (do not edit by hand) |
| `tests/cthulhu_*` | Scenario worldmods run by `tests/run.sh` (§6, [`tests/README.md`](../../tests/README.md)) |

Mineclonia framework references below are to `vendor/mineclonia/mods/ENTITIES/mcl_mobs/`.

## 1. Overview

| Stat | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| HP | 550 | Between Gojira (500) and Ghidorah (600). |
| Armor | `armor = 45` | He takes 45 % of punch damage. 550 HP are worth 1222 in water (Gojira 500 / 0.40 = 1250, Ghidorah 600 / 0.50 = 1200) and 815 on dry land, where every hit counts 1.5×. |
| Damage | 16 per claw swipe, every 1.5 s | Between Ghidorah's bite (13) and Gojira's (18). He has no ranged weapon; the grab only works on players and small mobs. |
| Reach | 8 | Measured from his centre; long arms (Gojira 7, Ghidorah 8). In sea mode from the nearest point of his body's axis (§3.5). |
| Size | `collisionbox = {-2.8, 0, -2.8, 2.8, 13, 2.8}`, `visual_size` 6 | 13 nodes, so he submerges fully in the 15-32 nodes of a v7 ocean. |
| Speed | `movement_speed = 7.0` on land, 8 nodes/s swimming at a target, 5 on the way home | Gojira: 7.5. |
| View | `view_range = 64`, `tracking_distance = 96` | The leash (§3.4), not the tracking distance, ends a chase. |
| Glow | `glow = 5` | A dim shape in deep water and in the darkness of his own aura. |
| Regeneration | 2 HP/s | While at least half submerged and 5 s after the last hit. |
| Drops | 1 heart of the sea, 8-16 prismarine crystals, 4-8 ink sacs; 250 XP | |
| Sounds | guardian, squid and wither sounds of `mobs_mc`, pitch 0.4 | Used by name, nothing is shipped. |
| Spawning | Egg only (`cthulhu:cthulhu`, creative inventory or `/giveme`) | `type = "monster"`, `_spawn_category = "misc"`, `persistent`. |

Balance against the other kaiju: in one exchange he is the weaker fighter. In
`cthulhu_kaiju` (§6) he and Gojira trade blows at the shore for 30 s: he loses about 160 HP
(bites of 8 and the fire breath), Gojira about 100 (swipes of 6.4). His strengths are the
sea: he regenerates there, Gojira can't follow him down, and nobody can target him under
water unless he attacks. On land he loses clearly (1.5× damage, 20 s limit). Long fights in his own water
have not been measured to the end.

## 2. Installation and settings

He is part of the `kaiju` modpack ([`../README.md`](../README.md)). Use the egg on the
shore or on the sea floor in shallow water: he picks the nearest water that is deep enough
to hide in (15 nodes), within 48 nodes, as his home (§3.4) and goes there. Without water
that is at least 8 nodes deep, the spot itself is his home, and he stands there in the
idol pose.

| Setting | Effect on him |
|---|---|
| `kaiju_smash_blocks` (default on) | Off: he no longer breaks the blocks in his way, on land or under water. |
| `mobs_griefing` gamerule | Off: the same. Protected areas are never touched. |

**On VoxeLibre** (0.92.4, through `kaiju_core/vl.lua`, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md#voxelibre)) home, territory, sea mode, grab, aura (nausea,
darkness), regeneration and the land weakness work as described here. On land he walks
straight lines (no pathfinder), `mobs_griefing` is a plain setting, and when he pulls a
player out of a boat he also clears the boat's driver (VoxeLibre's boats don't on detach).

### 3.1 Map of the file

| Mob definition | Stats, sounds, animation table, `kc.lock_animations` (animation lock) |
| Home and territory | `on_spawn`, `test_object_and_restriction` (leash, truce), `exposed`, `is_intruder`, `intruder_rule`, `get_active_target`, `valid_enemy` |
| Sea mode | `set_sea`, `swim_to`, `motion_step`, `target_box`, `swim_at`, `swim_home`, `walk_home` |

| Every step | `tick`, `run_ai`, `ai_functions`, hit boxes, `aura`, `ai_step` |

### 3.2 Per-step flow

`mob_class:on_step` calls, in this order:

1. `check_standin`: `kaiju_core.check_standin(…, math.huge)`. mcl_mobs never sees him in
   water (no swim physics, no bobbing, `floats = 0`), but the real depth is returned and
   stored in `_immersion_depth`.
2. `motion_step`: on land (and when dead) the stock one; in sea mode
   `kaiju_core.fly_velocity` towards `_goal`.
3. `ai_step`: stock, then hit boxes, `kaiju_core.smash_step` (both modes, but not while
   he rests at home without a target: he would wear the sea floor away under him) and,
   once a second while he has a target, `aura`.
4. `run_ai`: `tick` first (below). On land the stock `run_ai` with
   `ai_functions = {check_attack, walk_home}`; in sea mode `check_attack`, else `swim_home`.

`tick` does, every step:

- timers: animation lock, roar, grab cooldown;
- the water probe, twice a second: `_floor_y` and `_surface_y` of the column he is in
  (`kaiju_core.water_column` walks the nodes, so ice, lily pads or leaves above the water
  don't hide it);
- the mode switch (§3.3);
- regeneration and the flee flag (§3.7);
- giving up and the way home (§3.4);
- `grab_step` (§3.6).

### 3.3 Sea mode and land mode

One flag, `_at_sea`, switched in `tick` with hysteresis on `_immersion_depth`: on at 8
nodes of water above his feet (`SEA_ENTER`), off below 5 (`SEA_LEAVE`). `set_sea` cancels
navigation and a bulldoze and resets the melee state.

| | Sea mode | Land mode (shallows and land) |
|---|---|---|
| Physics | `kaiju_core.fly_velocity`: the velocity is blended towards `_goal`, no gravity. He stays `physical`, so the sea floor and the shore stop him. | Stock mcl_mobs physics. He wades at full speed. |
| Steering | `swim_at` (target) or `swim_home`. `gopath` does nothing. | `kaiju_core.update_bulldoze`, `gopath`, `end_bulldoze`, as Gojira. |
| Rising ground | `swim_to` probes the column 4 nodes ahead twice a second. If it is higher than the floor under him, `_floor` is set and `fly_velocity` climbs on the spot before he goes on. | `stepheight`, smashing. |
| Blocks | `smash_step` in both modes: he also breaks through reefs, wrecks and piers. The layer he stands on and natural terrain at foot level stay. | |

Depth in sea mode (`swim_at`, `swim_home`):

| Situation | Feet at |
|---|---|
| Idle at home, fleeing, returning: within 4 nodes of home | the sea floor (idol pose, once he touches the ground or is within 1.6 nodes of the floor of his column: on a slope he rests on the highest node under him) |
| On the way home | 2 nodes below "fully submerged" (`surface − 15`), or the floor where the water is shallower |
| Target whose top is less than 2 nodes under the surface (on land, in a boat, swimming, wading: a swimming Gojira's feet are 10 nodes down, his head is out) | `surface − 9`: head and shoulders (`HEAD_OUT` = 4 nodes) out of the water |
| Target fully under water: its top 2 nodes or more under the surface, and in water (a target in a dry pit below sea level, such as the one he digs into a shore, is not) | half his height below the target, never above `surface − 9` |

He stops `min(2.8 + target's half-width + 2, reach − 0.5)` nodes from the target, the
same gap as `kaiju_core`'s melee standoff. The target's collision box is read once per
target (`target_box`): `get_properties` copies every property.

### 3.4 Home, territory and targeting

**Home** (`on_spawn`, once): if the water at the spawn spot is at least 15 deep
(`HOME_DEPTH`: he cruises 2 nodes under "submerged"), that spot. Otherwise he probes 16
bearings on rings of radius 4, 8, … 48 around it and stops at the first ring with such a
column (the deepest one found up to there). So home is as near as possible, and the beach
the egg was used on is in his territory (`<= 48`); on a very flat shore it is only just.
If there is no such water within 48 nodes: the deepest column that is at least 8 deep, or
the spot itself. `_home` is a plain table `{x, y, z}` and `_home_depth` a number;
mcl_mobs saves both with the mob.

**Rules**, in priority order:

| # | Rule | Acquire | Keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `build_retaliation_target_rule({"cthulhu:cthulhu"})` | Whoever damaged him (not another Cthulhu), wherever it stands. | Stock: within `tracking_distance`, 15 s out of sight. |
| 2 | `intruder_rule` (own function) | Every 0.5 s: the nearest `is_intruder` object within 48 nodes of home, horizontally (`TERRITORY`), that he is not ignoring (below). **No line of sight needed**: from the sea floor the shore is hidden behind the slope. | Until it fails `test_object_and_restriction`. |

- `is_intruder`: players, and mobs that are not water creatures (`swims`,
  `breathes_in_water`, `amphibious`), not flyers and not another Cthulhu. And only where
  he can sense them, `exposed`: **under the open sky or in open water**. The natural light
  at noon (`core.get_natural_light(pos, 0.5)`) at the object, or above the water it is in,
  must be at least 8 (`OPEN_SKY`). That is the beach, the sea, a forest, a hut with a
  door; not a cave, a mine or a cellar, even right next to his home.
- Flyers: mcl_mobs has no common flag, so they are `airborne` (the parrot), `_flying` (a
  flying Ghidorah), Mothra, the names in `FLYERS` (bat, blaze, vex, ghast), and any mob
  with more than 3 nodes of air (`FLYER_HEIGHT`, down to ground or water) under its feet.
- `test_object_and_restriction` adds two things to the stock check, for every rule, for a
  new target and for the one he has. The **leash**: nobody further than 72 nodes from
  home (`LEASH`, horizontal) is a target. So an intruder is chased up to 72 nodes from
  home, and an attacker beyond it is not chased at all. The **truce**: a kaiju on a truce
  retreat (`_retreat_until`) is no target; hitting it would end the truce (see
  `kaiju_core`). One he is fighting when its retreat begins is let go.
- `get_active_target` returns nil while `_fleeing` or `_returning`, and forgets the
  target the rules had. Back home he only takes up whoever is in the territory or hits him.
- `valid_enemy` is false while he is fully under water (`pos.y + 13 < _surface_y`) **and
  has no target and holds no victim**. mcl_mobs asks it for every mob's target, so Gojira,
  Ghidorah, Mothra, golems and all others lose him when he dives away or sleeps. While he
  attacks he is always a valid enemy, also when he fights a diver under water: whoever he
  fights can fight back. Players can always hit him.

**Giving up.** Two timers run in `tick` while he has a target; either sets `_returning`:
he walks and swims home and ignores intruders until he is there (at most 60 s). A hit on
the way from someone within the leash ends it at once.

- **On land:** `_land_time` counts the time with less than 1 node of water around him and
  is reset in water. Limit 20 s (`LAND_LIMIT`).
- **No progress, anywhere:** `_stall_time` counts while the target is out of reach
  (`attack_dist` > reach + 1) and he has not come a node closer than ever before
  (`_stall_best`). It is reset in reach and during a grab. Limit 12 s (`STALL_LIMIT`):
  a player on a tower or behind something he may not break, a flyer that hit him. He
  then also ignores that object as an intruder for 60 s (`_ignored`, `IGNORE_TIME`), so
  he does not come straight back. If it hits him, retaliation still applies.

### 3.5 Fighting (`attack_null`)

1. In sea mode, `swim_at` steers (not while he holds a victim).
2. First step of a fight: if he was in the idol pose, the `rise` clip (3 s, locked) and a
   roar right after it.
3. While a clip is locked or a grab runs: nothing else.
4. Roar when `_roar_timer` has run out (every 20 s): `roar` clip (2 s), `war_cry` sound.
   On land he stands still for it.
5. Grab, if the target can be grabbed (§3.6).
6. Otherwise `mob_class.attack_melee` (the claw swipe, `punch` clip), on land after
   `kaiju_core.update_bulldoze`.

`pre_melee_attack`: mcl_mobs measures the reach from his feet. In sea mode they are 9 nodes
under the surface, so the distance is taken from the nearest point of the vertical axis of
his body instead (`attack_dist`). On land the stock measure stays, as `kaiju_core`'s
standoff expects it.

Against other kaiju he only has the claws: they are never grabbed.

### 3.6 Tentacle grab

An attached object's server position is its parent's origin, whatever the attach offset
(`player_sao.cpp`, `luaentity_sao.cpp`). So the victim is attached with zero offset to an
invisible entity of its own, `cthulhu:grip` (`static_save = false`, not pointable), and
that entity is moved. The victim's real position follows: drowning, other mobs' aim.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Start (`start_grab`) | Target passes `can_grab`, is within 10 nodes of his face, in sight, cooldown over. He stops and plays `grab` (ends in the hold pose). |
| 0.6 s (`grab_step`) | The claws close. If the victim is still within 13 nodes: a boat driver is detached from the boat (its `on_detach_child` tidies up, the boat stays), the grip is created at the victim's position, the victim attached. |
| Hold (grip `on_step`) | The grip moves at 8 nodes/s to `drag_point`: the point between the claws in the hold pose, lowered to 2 nodes under the surface if there is water at that spot (in the shallows the victim hangs in the air). The point must have room for the victim (`is_free`: no solid node at its feet or head). Facing the shore his claws are inside the bank; the point is then pulled back towards him (4, then 2.5 nodes forward: inside his own collision box, where nothing solid can be) and, failing that, lifted out. 2 damage per second. |
| Release | After 6 s; when he took 25 damage since the grab started (`_grab_damage`, summed in `receive_damage`); when he dies, flees or gives up; when the victim dies or is detached by something else; when the fight ends (`attack_end`); when the grip unloads (`on_deactivate`). On dry land the victim is put on the ground below instead of dropped. |
| After | 10 s cooldown. |

`can_grab`: players that mobs may attack (not creative, not peaceful), and mcl_mobs mobs
under 3 nodes tall that are not bosses and not kaiju. Attached victims are left alone,
except the driver of an `mcl_boats` boat.

`_grip` and `_grab_victim` are top-level fields: mcl_mobs drops ObjectRefs from the
staticdata only there. After a reload the grip is gone and `grab_step` ends the grab.

### 3.7 Aura, regeneration, land weakness, flight

- **Aura** (`aura`, once a second while he has a target or holds a victim): players within
  32 nodes of his head get `nausea` (3 s, mcl_potions: the colours turn; needs shaders or
  post-processing on the client), within 12 nodes also `darkness` (5 s, refreshed only
  when nearly over, because each refresh restarts its pulse). Creative players are spared.
- **Regeneration** (`tick`): `heal_mob(2 · dtime)` while `_immersion_depth ≥ 6.5` and
  `_recent_attacker` is nil (mcl_mobs clears it 5 s after a hit that has a source).
- **Land weakness** (`receive_damage`): damage × 1.5 while `_immersion_depth < 1`.
- **Flight**: below 25 % HP `_fleeing` is set, if home is deeper than he is tall. He has no
  target, lets go of a victim, goes home and rests on the floor until he is back at 70 %
  (125 s at 2 HP/s without being hit). After 180 s (`FLEE_TIMEOUT`) the flight ends
  whatever his HP: he could not get home, or could not heal there. If he is still below
  25 % he is `_cornered` and does not flee again until he is above it.
- `step_drowning` returns false.

### 3.8 Hit boxes, animation lock, death

- **Hit boxes** (`kaiju_core.ensure_hitboxes`): `chest` (0.5 forward, 9 up, 6 × 7 × 6) and
  `head` (4.2 forward, 7.5 up, 4.4 cube). Stand-pose positions; they are off in the idol
  pose, while swimming and in the roar.
- **Animation lock** (`kaiju_core.lock_animations`, `kaiju_core.play`): shared with
  Ghidorah. `rise`, `roar` and `grab`
  lock the animation for their length; only `die` gets through.
- **Death**: `kaiju_core.check_for_death` (plays `die`, stops him), then the grab ends. In
  sea mode a dead Cthulhu gets the stock physics back and sinks to the floor.

## 5. Model and texture pipeline

### 5.1 Files

| File | Built by |
|---|---|
| `models/cthulhu.glb` | `blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/cthulhu_model.py -- kaiju/cthulhu/models/cthulhu.glb` (Blender 5.x) |
| `textures/cthulhu.png` | `python3 tools/cthulhu_texture.py kaiju/cthulhu/textures/cthulhu.png` (PIL) |

Both scripts are deterministic (fixed seeds). The model script ends with a self-check
(vertex limit, bone list and parents, every bone along +Y with roll 0, feet planted in walk,
nothing below the ground in dormant) and prints the numbers below. Visual check:
`tests/run.sh cthulhu_show 40`.

Design sources: Lovecraft's 1934 sketch (profile, front, back) and the idol description of
"The Call of Cthulhu" (1928). Hunched, pear-shaped scaly body with a high hump; a bulbous
head hanging forward in front of the chest, with the mantle bulging up and back, a cluster
of three small eyes per side and a beard of 8 feelers; narrow folded wings whose arched
spars end in hooks above the head; big claws on hands and feet; no tail. Wet sea-green to
green-black with slime highlights and rare gold flecks.

### 5.2 Size

| | Value |
|---|---|
| `visual_size` | **`{x = 6, y = 6}`** (`SCALE = 6`): 1 model unit = 0.6 node |
| `collisionbox` | **`{-2.8, 0, -2.8, 2.8, 13, 2.8}`** |
| Height, stand | 21.5 units = **12.9 nodes** (the wing hooks, above the head) |
| Width, stand | 16.0 units = 9.6 nodes with the arms; belly 9.6 units = 5.8 nodes |
| Height, dormant | 12.3 nodes |
| Roar, wings spread | span 41.7 units = 25.0 nodes; 13.8 nodes high (the splayed feelers) |
| Die, lying | 9.3 nodes high (the wings), from 8.8 nodes behind to 8.9 nodes in front of the origin |

The collision box matches the belly (2.9 nodes half-width); only the hanging arms (4.8 nodes)
reach outside it. It is 13 nodes high so he can submerge in 15-32 node deep ocean. Only the
roar is higher (13.8 nodes).

### 5.3 Animations

`fps = 1` in the file, so frame numbers are seconds. Frame 0 is the neutral pose every
one-shot clip starts and ends in. Looping clips have identical first and last keys.

| Clip | Frames | Suggested fps | Loop | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `stand` | 0-60 | 15 | yes | Idle: the feelers writhe (waves run down each one, own phase per feeler), the mantle pulses, slight breathing, claws flex, wings twitch |
| `walk` | 70-110 | `walk_speed × v` | yes | One stride (left + right step), feet planted. Lumbering: he leans forward, shifts and rolls his hips over the stance foot (more on the right step: the stumble), the long arms swing against the legs, the head nods, the beard swings after it. `WALK_CYCLE_UNITS = 6.572` (3.94 nodes), so **`walk_speed = 40 / 3.94 = 10.1` frames per node** |
| `swim` | 120-160 | 16 | yes | Body pitched 70° forward with the belly 4.5 units above the origin, head up, alternating overarm strokes, leg kicks, feelers streaming back |
| `rise` | 170-230 | 20 | no | Starts in the `dormant` pose (170), lifts the head (180), pushes up from the knees (196), stands (210), feelers splay and wings half open (220), settles into `stand` (230) |
| `punch` | 240-260 | 25 | no* | Claw swipe with the `_l` arm: wind-up with open claws (242), hit across the body with closed claws (**245**), follow-through (249). Early hit, because mcl_mobs deals damage when the clip starts |
| `grab` | 270-300 | 20 | no | The `_r` arm reaches out with open claws (277), the claws close (**282**), pull the victim to the face (**290**, hold pose; feelers reach for it), back to neutral (300) |
| `roar` | 310-350 | 20 | no | The madness roar: ducks (314), then rears back with braced knees, head thrown up, claws up and wide open, **wings spread fully** (only here) and beating, feelers splayed radially and shivering, mantle swollen (hold 319-345) |
| `die` | 360-400 | 10 | no | Staggers (366), sags to his knees (376), falls forward (392) and lies face down with the mantle deflated (400) |
| `dormant` | 410-450 | 8 | yes | The idol pose: squatting on his heels, forearms on the knees, hands hanging over them, head bent, the feelers curl forward over the hands (nothing dips below the ground), wings folded with the hooks above the head. Only the feelers and mantle move |

\* As with Gojira, `punch_loop` may stay unset.

The mob plays `grab` only up to frame 290 (`grab_end = 290`, no loop), so the clip ends in
the hold pose and stays there until the grab is over (§3.6). `swim` is played while he
cruises; it is not synchronised with his speed.

```
│                           ├─ neck ─ head ─┬─ mantle               (back of the dome; scaled in stand/dormant/die)
│                           │               └─ feeler1a ─ feeler1b ─ feeler1c   … feeler8a ─ feeler8b ─ feeler8c
│                           ├─ wing_l1 ─ wing_l2        wing_r1 ─ wing_r2
│                           ├─ uarm_l ─ farm_l ─ hand_l ─ claws_l
│                           └─ uarm_r ─ farm_r ─ hand_r ─ claws_r
├─ thigh_l ─ shin_l ─ foot_l
└─ thigh_r ─ shin_r ─ foot_r
```

- `_l` = +X side = the mob's own **right**; `_r` = −X.
- Feelers 1-5 are the front row from +X to −X (3 is the centre one, the longest), 6-8 the
  back row from +X to −X. `a` is the root segment on the face, `c` the tip.
- `wing_X1`: the spar from the shoulder hump up to the hook. `wing_X2`: the blade and
  membrane from the hook down the back.
- The legs hang from `root`, not `hips` (as in Gojira and Ghidorah).

#### Overrides

**Every bone points along +Y with roll 0**, so at rest every bone's local axes are the
model axes. A relative `set_bone_override(bone, {rotation = {vec = v, absolute = false}})` is
applied after the animation, about the bone's own (= parent's posed) axes. Measured in the
client (`tests/cthulhu_show` scenes 12-15, Luanti 5.17), the same signs as Ghidorah's
`head_X`:

| `v` | Effect on `head` | Effect on a feeler (hangs down) |
|---|---|---|
| `x > 0` | nose **down** | swings it **back** (`x < 0` swings it forward, towards a target in front) |
| `y > 0` | rolls the +X side down | swings it towards −X |
| `z > 0` | nose turns towards **+X** (`_l`) | twists it about the vertical (barely visible) |

- **Head aim:** override `head`. Pitch and yaw towards a target: send
  `(-pitch, 0, -yaw_off)` with `aim_angles` as in Ghidorah's `aim_head`. The head joint is at (forward 2.40, side 0, up 7.56) nodes in `stand`.
- **Feeler lash:** override `feelerNa` (the whole feeler follows); add `feelerNb`/`feelerNc`
  for a curl. `set_bone_override` replaces a bone's whole override, so combine rotation and
  scale in one call.
- **Grab:** the animation moves the `_r` arm; for a different reach, override `uarm_r`
  (pitch: `x < 0` raises the hanging arm forward) and `claws_r` (`x > 0` curls the claws
  down/closed, `x < 0` opens them).

### 5.5 Grip point (grab attach point)

The point between the closed claws of the **`claws_r`** bone.

- **Bone-local** (for `set_attach(parent, "claws_r", pos, rot)`): Blender offset (0, 1.9, −1.2)
  from the `claws_r` joint = Luanti **`{x = 0, y = -1.2, z = 1.9}`** in mesh units. The
  parent's `visual_size` scales it (tested: the marker in scene 16 sits between the claws).
  The mob does not attach to the bone: an attached object's *server* position is the
  parent's origin, so the victim hangs on a grip entity placed with `model_point` (§3.6).
- **Model space**, for `kaiju_core.model_point(self, fwd, side, up)` (side + = +X / `_l`;
  the `_r` hand is on the − side):

| Pose | Frame | forward | side | up (nodes) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stand (claws hanging) | 0 | 4.44 | −3.48 | 2.57 |
| grab, reaching | 277 | 5.24 | −4.02 | 10.26 |
| grab, claws close | 282 | 6.89 | −3.69 | 7.95 |
| grab, hold at the face | 290 | 5.47 | −0.17 | 5.94 |

Other points (nodes): face centre (below the eyes, where the feelers start) in `stand`
(5.32, 0, 6.65), in the grab hold (4.90, 0, 5.36), in `roar` (3.26, 0, 10.67).

## 7. Known limitations and gotchas

1. **Straight lines only at sea.** Sea mode has no pathfinder. He swims straight at the
   goal, lifts over rising ground and breaks what he may break. An island of unbreakable
   or protected blocks between him and his goal stops him.
2. **Steep shores.** At a vertical shore he climbs out by lifting over the edge, or digs
   a ramp into it in land mode (the test sea has such walls). Sea and land mode can
   alternate a few times there.
3. **The swim clip slides.** `swim` is not tied to his speed, and a roar or a rise is
   played while he keeps moving in the water.
4. **Hit boxes use the stand pose** (§3.8).
5. **Nausea needs shaders** or post-processing on the client; darkness works without.
   Mineclonia's darkness is strong: within 12 nodes of him the screen is black, also at
   noon (`cthulhu_sea` screenshots), and only glowing things show. That is why he glows.
   Set `DARK_RADIUS = 0` to switch it off.
6. **The grip passes through nodes** on its way to the hold point: a victim grabbed
   across a shore edge is dragged through it (well under a second). The hold point itself
   is never inside a solid node (§3.6), and on release the victim is lifted out if it ends
   in one. Where the water at his claws is shallow, the victim is held in the air and
   does not drown.
7. **Land mobs include animals.** A cow on the beach within 48 nodes of his home wakes
   him. That is the approved design; he reaches and kills such mobs within seconds. Mobs
   under ground don't wake him, but "under the open sky" is a light test (§3.4): a mob
   a few nodes inside a cave mouth or in a hut with open doors still counts.
8. **A fleeing Cthulhu can still be hit by players.** Only mobs lose him under water.
   Hits delay the regeneration, so a diver with a trident can finish him.
9. **Flyers.** He ignores Mothra, a flying Ghidorah and other flyers as intruders,
   because he can't reach them. If they hurt him he still turns to them (retaliation), but
   gives up after 12 s out of reach (§3.4) and goes home; the next hit turns him again.
   Flying mobs of other mods are only recognised by the air under their feet.
10. **Unloading during a grab** is handled by the grip's `on_deactivate` and the engine's
    own detach; no scenario covers it.
11. **Balance is measured only in short fights** on the test sea's shore (§1).
12. **A flat shore puts home far out.** Water 15 deep can be 40-48 nodes from the beach
    (44 in `cthulhu_ocean`). The spot the egg was used on is then just inside his
    territory, and the land behind it is not. Use the egg at the waterline.
13. **In reach means no giving up.** Standing in water that is at least 1 node deep, with
    the target in reach, he fights on without a time limit (the land timer only counts on
    dry land, the stall timer only out of reach).

## 8. Interactions

| Who | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cthulhu → anyone | Retaliation; intruders in his territory, kaiju included, except flyers. Never a kaiju on a truce retreat, also not one he was already fighting | §3.4 |
| Gojira → Cthulhu | Retaliation, and `kaiju_core.cthulhu_rule()` (`build_nearest_target_rule("mob", {"cthulhu:cthulhu"}, nil, function() return 32 end)`) after it and before players: only within 32 nodes, in sight, and let go beyond 32 | `gojira/init.lua` |
| Ghidorah → Cthulhu | The same rule, after his hunt for Gojira and Mothra and before players. `kaiju_core.is_kaiju` includes Cthulhu, so the side heads beam him on their own | `ghidorah/init.lua` |
| Mothra → Cthulhu | `guards_against_cthulhu`, after retaliation: only while he is after someone other than her or holds a victim; dropped as soon as that ends. He is excluded from her general monster rule, and her gusts and powder spare him while he is calm | `mothra/init.lua` |
| All → a dived Cthulhu | Nobody while he has no target (asleep, diving away, on his way home): `valid_enemy` is false. While he attacks he can always be targeted | §3.4 |
| Truce | He takes no part in `kaiju_core.start_truce`, and his death starts none | |

The range of the 32-node rules must be a function: `build_nearest_target_rule` silently
ignores a number.
