# Gojira

A giant fire-breathing kaiju mob for [Mineclonia](https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia).
He is about 15.6 nodes tall, hunts players and iron golems, breathes fire, and
smashes through anything in his way, including forests, houses and hills, while
leaving the ground he stands on intact. He wades through lakes and swims in deep
water.

This document is for developers who want to change or extend the mob, or build
another kaiju on the same patterns (the three-headed King Ghidorah,
[`../ghidorah/README.md`](../ghidorah/README.md), was built on them). It
describes what the code does. Where a code comment says something different, the
code is documented and the difference is flagged with **Note:**.

Files (paths from the repo root; the mod ships in the `kaiju` modpack, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md)):

**Note:** the comment on `armor` says "takes 40% of melee damage". The armor group
only applies in `mob_class:on_punch` (`combat.lua:555-570`), that is, to player
melee, mob melee (`attack:punch`) and explosions (`on_blast` punches the mob,
`init.lua:434-440`). Damage dealt through `mcl_util.deal_damage` bypasses armor and
arrives at full strength. That includes bow arrows (`mcl_bows/arrow.lua:243`),
Mothra's gust and powder, and mob projectiles.

**Note:** `fire_damage = 0` and `lava_damage = 0` have no effect. Current mcl_mobs
only defines them as defaults and never reads them. `_fire_resistant = true` is
what blocks fire and lava damage (`physics.lua:433`), and `fire_resistant = true`
hides the burning sprites.

**Dependencies** (`mod.conf`): `mcl_mobs, mcl_burning, mcl_fire, mcl_util, mobs_mc, kaiju_core`,
with `min_minetest_version = 5.10` (the first release with glTF). `kaiju_core` (same
modpack) supplies smashing, bulldozing, wading, the knockback and death fixes and the
griefing check (`kaiju_core.griefing_enabled`, §2). `mobs_mc` supplies the sounds and the
iron golem, and the `mobs_griefing` check that `kaiju_core.griefing_enabled` builds on.
`mcl_fire` supplies the `fire_basic_flame.png` texture and the `mcl_fire:fire` node.
`mcl_util` and `mobs_mc` are not referenced directly in `init.lua`. The drop items come from `mcl_core` and
`mcl_mobitems`, which are not declared but always exist in Mineclonia.

**Spawning:** egg only. `mcl_mobs.register_egg("gojira:gojira", ...)`
(`init.lua:305`) registers no spawner. The egg is in the creative inventory.

**Persistence:** two settings keep him in the world.

- `persistent = true` means `despawn_allowed()` is never true (`spawning.lua:62-77`), and the mob caps don't count him (`update_mob_caps`, `spawning.lua:504-514`).
- `_spawn_category = "misc"` makes `register_mob` set `can_despawn = false` (`init.lua:388-396`).

**Peaceful difficulty:** `type = "monster"` defaults `persist_in_peaceful` to false.
The egg spawns him with `persist_in_peaceful = true` in the staticdata, so an
egg-spawned Gojira survives peaceful mode. A Gojira created with a plain
`core.add_entity` (as in the tests) is removed on activation in peaceful mode
(`api.lua:396`). He never attacks players on peaceful anyway
(`attack_player_allowed`, `combat.lua:401`).

## 2. Installation and usage

1. Gojira ships in the `kaiju` modpack. Installing, enabling and upgrading the modpack is described in [`../README.md`](../README.md).
2. His own dependencies are `kaiju_core` (same modpack) and Mineclonia's `mcl_mobs`, `mcl_burning`, `mcl_fire`, `mcl_util` and `mobs_mc` (§1). Without `kaiju_core` the world refuses to load gojira (missing dependency).
3. Spawn him with the egg item **`gojira:gojira`** (`/giveme gojira:gojira`, or take it from the creative inventory) by right-clicking a node. He appears on top of the clicked node (`pointed_thing.above`, y − 0.5). The egg respects protection at that position. Leave about 15 nodes of headroom; if there isn't enough, griefing will clear it.

### Settings that affect him

| Setting | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `mobs_griefing` | `/gamerule mobs_griefing false`, world.mt `mcl_mobs_griefing`, or global `mobs_griefing` | When false, he breaks no blocks (`kaiju_core.smash_step` returns before `smash`) and his flames start no fires, whatever the two `kaiju_*` settings below say. Movement and combat are unchanged. Without griefing he is a normal path-finding mob that can't fit through forests. |
| Per-mob griefing list | world.mt `mcl_mobs_griefing_disable_individual = gojira:gojira` (comma separated), or global `mobs_griefing_disable_individual` | Same as above for Gojira only (`mobs_mc/init.lua:15-20`, `mcl_init/init.lua:629-634`). There is no chat command for it (the setting is hidden). |
| `kaiju_smash_blocks` | modpack setting, default `true` (`kaiju/settingtypes.txt`; Luanti's settings menu or `minetest.conf`) | When false, `kaiju_core.smash_step` does nothing, for every kaiju: no body smash and no tail sweep. His flames still ignite blocks. Movement is as with `mobs_griefing` off. |
| `kaiju_gojira_fire` | modpack setting, default `true` (same place) | When false, the flame's `hit_node` no longer places `mcl_fire:fire`. Mobs and players hit by a flame still take the damage and burn. Smashing is unchanged. |
| Protection | any protection mod | Smashing (`kaiju_core`) and flame ignition call `core.is_protected(pos, "")`. The empty player name means protected areas are never touched. |
| `mobs_drop_items` | gamerule | Death drops. |
| `mcl_doTileDrops` | gamerule (`/gamerule do_tile_drops false`) | Turns off all node drops from smashing. A chest's **contents** still drop (`after_dig_node`), only the chest item itself does not. |
| Difficulty | `/difficulty` | Peaceful: he ignores players (see §1). |
| Creative mode | per player | He ignores creative players (`attack_player_allowed`). |

The two `kaiju_*` settings apply on top of `mobs_griefing`, never instead of it:
`kaiju_core.griefing_enabled(mob_name, setting)` returns
`core.settings:get_bool(setting, true) and mobs_mc.is_mob_griefing_enabled(mob_name)`.
It is called on every check (each `smash_step`, each flame hitting a node), so the
settings are read live and a change needs no restart. The third setting in
`kaiju/settingtypes.txt`, `kaiju_ghidorah_beam_blasts`, does not concern Gojira.

**On VoxeLibre** (0.92.4, through `kaiju_core/vl.lua`, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md#voxelibre)) he does all of the above, with these differences:
he walks straight at his target instead of following a path (smashing and bulldozing as
usual), `mobs_griefing` is a plain setting there (no per-mob list), the 5 % drops follow
`mcl_doTileDrops`, VoxeLibre's arrows also hit his body (not only the hit boxes), and lava,
fire nodes, drowning and fall damage don't affect him (he is fire resistant and holds his
breath on Mineclonia anyway).

### 3.2 Per-step flow

`mob_class:on_step` (`api.lua:542-724`) runs once per server step (0.05 s:
Mineclonia sets `dedicated_server_step = 0.05`, `minetest.conf:36`). In order, it
does the following (Gojira's hooks in bold):

1. Despawn, mob-cap and proto-chunk checks (`check_proto_chunk` pauses the whole AI while the mapchunk is still being generated).
2. Computes `standing_in`/`standing_on`, then fall damage and dying.
3. **`check_standin`** (`kaiju_core.check_standin`) decides wade or swim and sets `floats`. The base method also applies environmental damage (`apply_environment_damage`) with the *unmodified* liquid type (§3.7).
4. `navigation_step`, `movement_step`, `motion_step`: path following, `go_to_stupidly` targets, physics. Physics uses `_last_liquidtype`, which is the liquid type **Gojira's** `check_standin` returned in the previous step.
5. `post_motion_step`, `update_timers`.
6. **`ai_step`** runs the base bookkeeping, keeps his two hit boxes alive (`kaiju_core.ensure_hitboxes`, §3.5), then **smashing** (`kaiju_core.smash_step`, §3.5).
7. `rotate_step`, `set_animation_speed` (the walk fps follows the velocity), head swivel, random sound.
8. `run_targeting_rules` picks `_active_target` from `_targeting_rules` (§3.3).
9. `run_ai` runs `ai_functions` in order until one returns non-nil:
   - `mob_class.check_attack` (`combat.lua:1244`). If there is no `self.attack` and an active target exists, it sets `self.attack`, `self.attacking = false`, and returns `"attack"`. On later steps it calls **`attack_melee`** (`attack_type = "melee"`). When the target is gone or changes, it calls **`attack_end`**.
   - `retreat` (between the two): during a truce (§8) it walks him straight to his retreat goal with `go_to_stupidly(goal, 1)` and the walk animation, and nothing after it runs. It first cancels a pathfinder path that is still set (`waypoints` or `pathfinding_context`): mcl_mobs' `navigation_step` follows those before the straight-line goal, so a Gojira who was on his way to Ghidorah used to walk on to the corpse (up to about 40 nodes the wrong way) before he turned round.
   - `mob_class.check_pace` (`movement.lua:1383`): random wandering at `pace_bonus` (0.5 × 7.5) after more than 5 s idle (1 in 120 chance per step). It calls `gopath`, which goes through the **override**.

The default `ai_functions` list of other mobs (avoiding, following, breeding and
so on) is replaced on purpose by these entries.

### 3.3 Targeting rules (in priority order)

`run_targeting_rules` (`combat.lua:13`) asks each rule in order and takes the
first valid object. The rule that produced the current target is called with
`is_current = true` and only checks whether to keep it. Rules earlier in the list
are still asked every step, so they can take over.

| # | Rule | Acquire | Keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", {"ghidorah:ghidorah"}, nil, nil, true)` | The nearest King Ghidorah within `view_range` (58) **with line of sight**. | `esp = true`: no line-of-sight check while keeping him. Kept while within 64 nodes. |
| 2 | `build_retaliation_target_rule()` | The last attacker (`_last_attacker`, set by `receive_damage` for any damage with a source, including Mothra's) within `tracking_distance`. No line-of-sight check. | Within 64 nodes. Keeps the target out of sight for up to 15 s (`RETALIATION_PERSISTENCE`). |
| 3 | `kaiju_core.cthulhu_rule()`, i.e. `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", {"cthulhu:cthulhu"}, nil, function() return 32 end, nil)`, shared with Ghidorah | The nearest Cthulhu within **32 nodes** with line of sight, and not while Cthulhu is fully under water without a target (his `valid_enemy`). The range must be a function: a number is silently ignored. | Within 32 nodes; lost after 3 s out of sight. See [`../cthulhu/README.md`](../cthulhu/README.md) §8. |
| 4 | `build_nearest_target_rule("player", nil, nil, nil, true)` | The nearest player within `view_range` (58) **with line of sight**, re-checked every 0.5 s (`check_timer("seek_target")`). Excludes creative players, peaceful difficulty and dead players. | `esp = true`: `persistence = nil`, so **no line-of-sight check while keeping** him. Kept while within 64 nodes and attackable. |
| 5 | `build_nearest_target_rule("mobs_mc:iron_golem", {"mobs_mc:iron_golem"})` | The nearest iron golem within 58 nodes with line of sight. | Lost after 3 s out of sight (`SIGHT_PERSISTENCE`). |
| 6 | `build_alert_receiver_rule()` | A target pushed by another mob's `broadcast_attack`. No current mob alerts Gojira, so this is a hook for future kaiju (§8). | 72 nodes for 20 s, then 64 nodes. |

Line of sight (`mob_class:target_visible`, `movement.lua:5`) is traced through nodes
from his eye (`head_eye_height`, derived by `register_mob` as 0.75 × 14.5 = 10.875
nodes) to the target's eye. Gojira does not target Mothra unless she damages him
(rule 2). Ghidorah is the archenemy and comes first, even over whoever is hitting him:
once a Ghidorah is in sight, Gojira drops a player or a Mothra for him (`kaiju_triangle`,
§6, shows `target=ghidorah` from t = 1).

**Truce.** While he retreats after a Ghidorah's death (§8), `gojira.get_active_target =
kaiju_core.get_active_target` hides whatever the rules chose, so he has no target at all.
A living Ghidorah is the exception: he ends the retreat and is the target again.

Two different "ESP" flags are involved:
- The `esp` argument of `build_nearest_target_rule` keeps the *target* without line of sight.
- `_melee_esp = true` lets base `attack_melee` keep *re-pathing* (`gopath`) without line of sight (`combat.lua:940`).

Neither allows a bite or fire breath without line of sight: `pre_melee_attack`
(`combat.lua:901`) and the breath check both require it. Behind a wall he walks or
bulldozes into it and smashes it.

### 3.4 `attack_melee`: fire breath, melee, bulldozing

`gojira:attack_melee(attach_pos, self_pos, dtime, target_pos, line_of_sight)` (lines 165-203):

```
cooldown -= dtime
if _breath_time:            -- BREATHING (state lasts BREATH_TIME = 2.5 s)
    look_at(target); every FLAME_INTERVAL: shoot_flame + breath_particles
    (the first flame waits BREATH_WINDUP = 0.8 s: _flame_timer starts at 0.8)
    on expiry: cooldown = 7 s; attacking = nil; set_animation("walk")
    return                  -- no pathing, no bite, no stuck detection
if cooldown == 0 and line_of_sight and 9 <= |attach_pos - target_pos| <= 32:
    START BREATH: _breath_time = 2.5, _flame_timer = 0.8,
    _punch_animation_timeout = nil, halt_in_tracks(true), cancel_navigation(),
    _breath_up = target_above(self, target_pos)
    set_animation(_breath_up and "breath_up" or "breath"), sound "shoot_attack"; return
kaiju_core.update_bulldoze(...)   -- stuck detection
return mob_class.attack_melee(...)   -- stock pathing + bite
```

Details:
- `_punch_animation_timeout = nil` is required. While it is above 0, `set_animation` forces `"punch"` (`effects.lua:221-225`), so `"breath"`/`"breath_up"` would never play.
- `self.attacking = nil` at the end of the breath makes base `attack_melee` re-initialise next step (`combat.lua:907-915`). That resets `_gopath_delay`, `_attack_delay` and `_target_pos`, so he repaths immediately.
- The breath distance is 3D from `attach_pos` (his feet). Between 7 (`reach`) and 9 (`BREATH_MIN_DIST`) nodes he does neither: he walks closer.
- The breath does not stop when the target moves out of range or sight. Once started, it runs the full 2.5 s while he turns towards the target.
- **Flame count:** at 20 steps/s, the first flame comes at 0.8 s, then one every 0.1 s (2 steps) until 2.5 s. That makes about 18 flames, each with 2 damage and 6 s of burning.

**Up or down breath** (`target_above`, lines 131-137). The direction is decided
**once**, at breath start, and stored in `self._breath_up` for the whole breath:

```lua
local from = mouth_pos(self, false)                        -- the normal (down) mouth
local cb = self.attack:get_properties().collisionbox
local dy = target_pos.y + (cb[2] + cb[5]) / 2 - from.y     -- target's box centre
local flat = horizontal distance from the mouth to target_pos
return math.atan2(dy, flat) > BREATH_UP_ANGLE              -- 15°
```

- If the elevation of the target's centre, seen from the normal mouth (10.4 nodes up), is above 15°, he plays `breath_up` and flames start at the up-pose mouth (`MOUTH_UP_FORWARD`/`MOUTH_UP_HEIGHT`, 2.53 / 15.76 nodes). Otherwise he plays `breath` from the normal mouth (6.32 / 10.40 nodes).
- Typical cases:
  - A ground target at 9-32 nodes is below the mouth (dy < 0), so he uses `breath`.
  - Mothra at about 25 nodes altitude uses `breath_up` (verified in `kaiju_fight`, §6).
  - A player on a pillar higher than about 10.4 + 0.27·distance nodes also uses `breath_up`.
- `mouth_pos(self, up)` takes the flag. `shoot_flame` and `breath_particles` pass `self._breath_up`.
- The flames still aim straight at the target either way. The pose only moves the origin and the visuals.
- `_breath_up` is never cleared (not at breath end, not in `attack_end`). That is harmless because every breath start overwrites it. It is also saved in staticdata (§7.16).

**Base melee** (`mob_class.attack_melee`, `combat.lua:905-974`) repaths every
0.2-0.55 s, plus 0.25 s beyond 16 nodes and 0.5 s beyond 32, and bites when
`distance <= reach`, the delay has elapsed and there is line of sight.
`custom_attack` plays `"punch"` and deals the damage **in the same step**, which is
why the model's bite snap is early (frame 126, §5.4). `_punch_animation_timeout` =
20/25 − 0.09 = 0.71 s.

#### Fire breath projectile (`gojira:flame`, lines 34-58)

- Registered through `mcl_mobs.register_arrow`, so the generic arrow `on_step` (`init.lua:529-647`) applies.
- **`_lifetime = 80` is counted in server steps**: `self.timer = self.timer + 1` once per `on_step`, and the flame is removed when `timer > _lifetime`. At 20 steps/s that is 4 s × 22 nodes/s = 88 nodes, far beyond `BREATH_MAX_DIST`. On a lagging server it lasts longer in seconds. The mcl_mobs default is 1500 steps.
- `velocity = 22` nodes/s. `shoot_flame` sets the velocity itself, aiming from `mouth_pos` at the target position plus a random offset of ±1 node in x and z and +0.8 to +1.8 in y (`aim`), so the flame aims at the body, not the feet.
- `ent.switch = 1` is required. A newly added arrow has `switch = 0` and removes itself in its first step. `owner_id = tostring(self.object)` stops the flames from hitting Gojira himself (`hit_mob` checks `owner_id`). `_shooter = self.object` makes `get_arrow_damage_func(2, "fireball")` report Gojira as the damage source. This is what triggers the victim's retaliation rule (for example Mothra's).
- `hit_player` and `hit_mob` both call `burn`: 2 damage of type `fireball` through `mcl_util.deal_damage`, then `mcl_burning.set_on_fire(obj, 6)`. The flames hit **every** mob except Gojira, including villagers, golems and Mothra.
- `hit_node` runs when the flame's current node is walkable. It ignites `round(pos − normalize(v))`, roughly the node the flame came from. It only does so if that node is air or `buildable_to`, `kaiju_core.griefing_enabled("gojira:gojira", "kaiju_gojira_fire")` is true (the `kaiju_gojira_fire` setting and `mobs_griefing`, §2), and the position is not protected. Mineclonia's `enable_fire` gamerule is not consulted.
- `_is_fireball = true` makes the flame entity itself burn (visual only). `glow = 14`, sprite 1.5 × 1.5.
- `breath_particles` adds one spawner per flame: 25 particles over 0.2 s at 18 nodes/s towards the target. Spawners overlap (0.2 s lifetime, 0.1 s interval), which makes the stream continuous.

#### Bulldozer (lines 201, 205-213; mechanics in `kaiju_core`)

The A* pathfinder rarely finds a route for a 5 × 15 node body through trees or
towns. Gojira wires up the shared bulldozer
([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.3): `attack_melee` calls
`kaiju_core.update_bulldoze`, `gojira:gopath` forwards to `kaiju_core.gopath`, and
`attack_end` calls `kaiju_core.end_bulldoze`. In short: after 3 s without getting more
than 1 node closer (horizontally), he walks straight at the target for 6 s with
`go_to_stupidly(target_pos, pursuit_bonus)` (`movement_speed` × 1.0), and `smash`
clears the way. Gojira-specific behaviour:

- `update_bulldoze` is not called during the fire breath, so the bulldoze timer **pauses** while he breathes. `gojira_water` shows a bulldoze lasting about 8.5 s = 6 + 2.5.
- "Close enough" uses his `reach + 1` = 8 nodes.
- **Melee standoff.** The same two calls (`update_bulldoze`, `gopath`) stop him when his body is 2 nodes from his target's, instead of walking to its feet as stock melee does: 4.7 nodes from a player, 6.5 (his reach − 0.5) from a Ghidorah, measured between the centres; he needs about a node to stop. Only for targets at body height, so he still walks under a flying Mothra. And only while his bite reaches (3D distance ≤ `reach`): below a target that hovers at chest height, such as a swimmer over deep water or a player on a low roof, he walks on until it does (`gojira_overhead`); he used to stand there, out of bite range and too close for the fire breath. Before this he and Ghidorah stood inside each other for a whole fight; `kaiju_clinch` now shows 5.5-6.4 nodes between them ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.3).
- `attack_end` (lines 209-213) clears `_breath_time`, then calls `kaiju_core.end_bulldoze` (drops a running bulldoze and its straight-line `stupid_target`, resets stuck detection), then the base `attack_end` (which releases the wielded item). Verified by `gojira_punch`: one second after the target is lost, `_bulldoze_time=nil stupid_target=nil`.

### 3.5 Block smashing (lines 227-248; mechanics in `kaiju_core`)

```lua
gojira._smash = {lookahead = 3, tail = {segments = TAIL, unit = UNIT, first = TAIL_FIRST}}
function gojira:ai_step(dtime)
	mob_class.ai_step(self, dtime)
	kaiju_core.smash_step(self, dtime, "gojira:gojira")
end
```

`kaiju_core.smash_step` smashes every 0.25 s (every 2 s while he idles without a
target), unless he is dead or `kaiju_core.griefing_enabled("gojira:gojira", "kaiju_smash_blocks")`
is false (the `kaiju_smash_blocks` setting or `mobs_griefing`, §2). One pass clears
his collision box stretched 3 nodes ahead, from the layer he stands on to one node above
his head, keeps that floor layer (except containers, beds and doors, which get a real
`dig_node`, wrapped in `pcall`, so chests drop their contents; §7.4) and natural terrain at foot level (so he climbs
hills), skips immune and protected nodes, lets sand fall and makes one dust cloud.
Full rules, constants and gotchas: [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md)
§3.2 and §6.

Gojira's numbers:

- **Box:** his collision box is 4.8 × 14.5 × 4.8, so with the 3-node lookahead the box is roughly 6-9 × 6-9 × 17 nodes, about 900 `get_node` calls per pass. The floor layer is `round(pos.y − 0.5)`, the top `round(pos.y + 14.5 + 1)`.
- **Lookahead 3:** at about 6.1 n/s he covers 1.5 nodes per 0.25 s tick, so the box always reaches past where he will be at the next tick.
- **Swimming** (`floats == 1`, deeper than `SWIM_DEPTH`): the floor layer and the terrain at foot level are dug too, so he cuts through a steep shore (§3.7).
- `gojira_smash` checks the result: the chest's 17 gold and 5 emerald end up on the ground, `floating_sand=0`, and `under=` is never air.

### 3.6 Tail sweep (lines 221-230)

The server doesn't know the animated pose, so the tail is a fixed line of boxes
straight behind him, built from the model's rest-pose tail. `kaiju_core` sweeps it
whenever he **turned** since the last smash pass (algorithm and limits:
[`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.2 "Tail sweep" and §6.6).
`TAIL[i] = {distance behind, centre height, thickness, plate height}` in model units,
converted with `unit = UNIT` (0.75 n per u):

| Column | Derived from (`tools/gojira_model.py`) |
|---|---|
| distance behind | −(midpoint y of `TAIL_PTS[i]`, `TAIL_PTS[i+1]`) |
| centre height | midpoint z of the same two points |
| thickness | `TAIL_W[i]` |
| plate height | `2.8 − 0.3·(i−1)`, the height of the tail's dorsal plate (line 182) |

Example: segment 1 runs from (0, −2.4, 8.6) to (0, −5.4, 8.2). The midpoint is
y −3.9, z 8.4, giving `{3.9, 8.4, 4.8, 2.8}`.

The library accepts an optional fifth value per segment, a side offset: such a segment
is swept twice, that far to the left and to the right of the centre line (Ghidorah's two
splayed tails). Gojira's segments have four values, so his one tail is swept on the
centre line.

`TAIL_FIRST = 3`: segments 1-2 (3.9 and 6.9 u = 2.9 and 5.2 n behind him) stay inside the
path his own body box already cleared, so only segments 3-8 are swept. With his tail,
`TAIL_YAW_STEP` (0.12 rad) is about 2 nodes at the tip (23.75 u = 17.8 n behind him).
For Gojira the library limits mean: turns slower than about 6.9°/s never sweep, turns
faster than 0.72 rad per 0.25 s leave gaps at the tip, and the stand/walk sway is ignored.

### 3.7 Water: wade vs. swim (lines 254-266; mechanics in `kaiju_core`)

```lua
local SWIM_DEPTH = 10
function gojira:check_standin(dtime, attach_pos)
	return kaiju_core.check_standin(self, dtime, attach_pos, SWIM_DEPTH)
end
```

`kaiju_core.check_standin` ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.4)
hides water shallower than `SWIM_DEPTH` from mcl_mobs' physics and switches `floats`:

- **Wading** (`depth < SWIM_DEPTH = 10` nodes of water above his feet anywhere in his collision box): land physics at full speed, `floats = 0`, and smash treats him as standing.
- **Swimming** (`depth >= 10`): swim physics with `water_velocity = 3.0`, and `floats = 1`. `motion_step` (`physics.lua:1132-1136`) then sets `jumping` on 70 % of steps while immersion is above 0.4, and that buoyancy lifts him. Because the jump stops when the depth falls under 10 (wading again, gravity), he settles at about depth 10 with his eyes (10.875) just above the surface.
- `floats = 0` in the definition is only the initial value.
- Water still extinguishes him (the base method applies environment damage with the real liquid type). The drowning check there (`immersion > eye height`) would apply, but `step_drowning` (lines 244-246) is overridden to `return false`. He holds his breath indefinitely (the base `respire` is skipped too).
- `gwp_penalties` (lines 265-266) is a copy of the class table with `WATER = 0.0` (default 8.0), so the pathfinder doesn't detour around lakes. `register_mob` picks it up from the definition (`init.lua:425-426`).

Measured in `gojira_water`, run on this checkout:
- Bulldozing through deep water at immersion 10.0-10.2: about 5.0-5.3 nodes/s, because he keeps toggling between swim and land physics around the threshold.
- Leaving the deep pool, he cut a flooded trench through the shore and the shallow pool's bed **at his swim level** (feet about 10 nodes below the surface). He then waded along it at immersion 9-10 and climbed out step by step (feet y −1.5 → 4.5 over about 10 s).

### 3.8 Death (line 300; mechanics in `kaiju_core`)

```lua
gojira.check_for_death = kaiju_core.check_for_death
```

`kaiju_core.check_for_death(self, reason, damage)` wraps the base method
(`physics.lua:178`) and adds two things:

- **`die` instead of a leftover bite.** On a fatal hit (`damage > 0` and `self.health <= 0`) it clears `_punch_animation_timeout` before the base method runs. While that timeout is above 0, `set_animation` turns every animation into `"punch"` (§7.5), so a Gojira killed within 0.71 s of a bite never played `"die"`.
- **He stops.** On the call that makes him dead (`self.dead` turns true) it calls `halt_in_tracks(true)`. mcl_mobs lets a dying mob walk on, so a Gojira killed mid-stride slid through his whole 3 s `die` clip.

The return value is the base method's.

## 5. Model and texture pipeline

### 5.1 Conventions (`tools/gojira_model.py:1-10`)

- Blender is Z-up and the model faces **+Y** (entity forward in Luanti). `export_yup=True` converts to glTF Y-up.
- **10 Blender units = 1 node at `visual_size` 1.** The mob uses `visual_size = 7.5`, so 1 unit = 0.75 node. The model is about 21 units tall (head plates at z ≈ 20.9), so about 15.6 nodes. From toe claws (y ≈ 5.4) to tail tip (y ≈ −25.4) he is about 30.8 units, 23 nodes. The collision box covers only the torso column. The tail sticks out 18.75 nodes behind it, so the tail is neither collidable nor pointable.
- **`scn.render.fps = 1`**, so a glTF timestamp in seconds equals the Blender frame number. Lua can use b3d-style frame ranges (`animation.walk_start = 70`) and choose any fps through `*_speed`. Luanti's legacy `set_animation` (which mcl_mobs uses) treats glTF frames as seconds (`doc/lua_api.md`, glTF section).
- **Rigid per-box skinning:** every box belongs 100 % to one bone (`vertex_groups[b].add([i], 1.0)`), and boxes overlap at joints to hide gaps. Weights are trivially normalized, as Luanti requires.
- All clips live in **one action** ("gojira") and are separated by frame ranges. mcl_mobs drives the legacy single-track `set_animation`.

### 5.5 Walk-cycle math (lines 293-328)

```
A        = 22°                         stride half-angle
LEG_V    = ankle − hip at rest         (y −0.4, z −6.9)
LEG_R    = hypot(LEG_V.y, LEG_V.z)     = 6.912 u
LEG_PHI  = atan2(−LEG_V.y, −LEG_V.z)   ≈ 3.3°, thigh pitch that puts the ankle under the hip
WALK_CYCLE_UNITS = 2 steps × 2·LEG_R·sin A = 10.356 u  (7.77 nodes)
```

Phase `p` per leg (right leg offset by π):
- **Stance** (`0 ≤ p ≤ π`): `rel = asin(sin A · (1 − 2p/π))`. The ankle's horizontal offset `LEG_R·sin(rel)` moves **linearly** from +sin A to −sin A, so the sole slides back at constant speed, like the body moves. Anything else would visibly slide. The root drops by `drop = −LEG_V.z − LEG_R·cos(rel)` (the arc's lift, max 0.49 u at footfall, about 0 mid-stance). That keeps the stance sole planted and makes the bob: lowest at footfall, highest at mid-stance.
- **Swing** (`π < p < 2π`): `rel = A·cos p` (eased return), `swing = −sin p` (0 → 1 → 0). The thigh gets +12°·swing, the shin −38°·swing (knee bend), and the foot `−(thigh + shin) + 10°·swing` (sole flat, toes lift).

Since the cycle covers `WALK_CYCLE_UNITS` per 40 frames, `walk_speed = 40 /
(WALK_CYCLE_UNITS·UNIT)` frames per node, times the velocity gives the fps that
keeps the feet planted.

**Density-based UVs** (`face_uvs`, lines 196-212): each face gets texels at

### 5.8 glTF export options (lines 380-388)

### Harness quirks

- **Targets are only acquired with line of sight** (§3.3). Scenarios spawn him in the open and build walls or pools only after `e.attack` is set (`wait()` loops in `gojira_smash`/`gojira_water`). A scene built first would leave him idle.
- **Spawn after the map is loaded.** Scenarios spawn and build inside `core.after(1..4)` from `on_joinplayer`. Before that the mapblocks may not exist: `add_entity` can fail, `bulk_set_node` is dropped, and a mob in an ungenerated chunk has its AI suspended (`init_ai` → `_in_proto_chunk`, `movement.lua:1124-1133`).
- **Timing:** singleplayer runs server and client in one process, so `os.clock()` (CPU time of the whole process, including the client's render thread) overstates server cost. Use `core.get_us_time()` (wall clock), as `gojira_tail` does.
- **Parallel runs** need distinct ports. `run.sh` derives one from its PID.
- Tests that override behaviour wrap the registered entity table (`core.registered_entities["gojira:gojira"].attack_melee = …`, or `mcl_mobs.mob_class.ai_step` in `register_on_mods_loaded`). Entities look methods up through it, so this works for mobs spawned later.
- `persist_in_peaceful` is false for `add_entity` spawns. Don't run scenarios on peaceful difficulty.

## 7. Known limitations and gotchas

Status: **[code]** = verified by reading the code; **[test]** = observed in a test run; **[unverified]** = plausible but not checked.

1. **Mineclonia's `mcl_mobs/API.md` is outdated** [code]. It documents `walk_velocity`, `run_velocity` and the `dogshoot` attack type, none of which exist in the code. Use `movement_speed` with the `*_bonus` factors, `_targeting_rules` and `ai_functions` instead. Trust the Lua.
2. **Projectile `_lifetime` is counted in server steps, not seconds** [code]. `init.lua:532-536` of mcl_mobs increments `timer` by 1 per `on_step`. Mineclonia runs at 20 steps/s (`dedicated_server_step = 0.05`), so 80 steps is 4 s, and longer under lag.
3. **Arrow hit detection has gaps** [code; impact unverified]. Each step raycasts only `velocity × 0.04 s` ahead while the flame moves `velocity × 0.05 s` (1.1 nodes), and `hit_node` checks only the node at the current position. Thin targets or wall corners can be skipped.
4. **Chests drop their contents only through `dig_node`/`after_dig_node`** [code]. `kaiju_core` digs containers, doors and beds with `dig_node` for that reason; see [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.2. The call has no digger, and some dig callbacks assume a player (`mcl_beehives` indexes the digger's inventory) and raise an error. `core.dig_node` is therefore wrapped in `pcall`: the error is logged once per node name as a warning (`[kaiju_core] dig callback of <node> failed: …`) instead of crashing the server, and the node counts as smashed if it is gone afterwards.
5. **`mcl_mobs:set_animation` only plays names present in the `animation` table** [code, `effects.lua:227-232`]. Both `<name>_start` and `<name>_end` must exist, otherwise the call is silently ignored. `breath_up` works only because `breath_up_start`/`_end` are in the table. While `self.attack` is set and `_punch_animation_timeout > 0`, any animation is forced to `"punch"`. Non-walk clips play at a fixed `<name>_speed`, while walk scales with velocity.
6. **Target acquisition needs line of sight; the `esp` flag only helps once a target is acquired** [code]. See §3.3. `_melee_esp` only affects re-pathing. Bites and breath always need line of sight.
7. **Selection box and server raycasts** [code in Luanti `src/serverenvironment.cpp:1391-1398`; arrows: test `gojira_arrows`]. Server-side `core.raycast` only considers objects whose *base position* is within 5 nodes of the ray's bounding box. Rays that pass through his upper body (more than about 5 nodes above his feet) miss him. For bow arrows and punches this is fixed by the chest and head hit boxes (§3.5). Mob projectiles (`mcl_mobs.register_arrow`) ignore hit boxes and Ghidorah's beams skip them, so those still have to pass within 5 nodes of his feet. In multiplayer, `checkInteractDistance` measures punches to his base position (feet), so punches aimed high from a pillar are rejected as too far (`serverpackethandler.cpp:950-975`, not in singleplayer). The selection box equals the collision box, so the head (above 14.5 nodes) and the tail are not pointable at all.
8. **The server doesn't know the animated pose** [code]. Hit boxes, `mouth_pos`, the smash box and the tail sweep all use fixed rest-pose geometry. Flames always start at the frame-166 (or frame-266, `breath_up`) mouth position, and the yaw sweep of the head is visual only. Flames aim straight at the target regardless of head pitch.
9. **Full knockback resistance zeroes velocity (fixed)** [code + test]. With `knockback_resistance = 1.0` alone, mcl_mobs sets a zero vector as his velocity on every full punch and every arrow. `kaiju_core.no_knockback(gojira)` (line 304) prevents that; the mechanism is in [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.5 and §6.8. `gojira_punch` shows the velocity unchanged after punches, and 0.00 with the stock path; damage is unchanged.
10. **Armor only applies to punches** [code], see §1.
11. **`_bulldoze_time` after target loss (fixed)** [code + test]: `attack_end` clears it and the straight-line goal, see §3.4.
12. **Leaving deep water he tunnels at swim depth** [test], see §3.7.
13. **Tail-sweep blind spots** [code]: slow turns (< 6.9°/s) and fast turns (> 0.72 rad per tick), see §3.6 and [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.6.
14. **Random smash drops and `mcl_doTileDrops` (fixed)** [code]: they check `mcl_vars.do_tile_drops`, see §2 and [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.3. No test covers the gamerule being off.
15. **Smash cost** [test]: average 4 ms and worst case about 112 ms per smash tick in `gojira_tail` (dense pillars while turning). It scales with box volume (about 900 `get_node` calls per tick) plus the tail sweep. Several Gojiras at once multiply it.
16. **Staticdata keeps transient state** [code]. `get_staticdata_table` serializes every non-function field (`api.lua:123-175`), including `_breath_time`, `_breath_up`, `_breath_cooldown`, `floats`, `attacking` and the `kaiju_core` fields (`_bulldoze_time`, `_stuck_timer`, `_smash_idle`, ...). After a reload these resume, for example a half-finished breath continues when the next target is attacked.
17. **Big collision box** [unverified]. He can't enter 1-wide gaps, and with griefing disabled (`mobs_griefing` or `kaiju_smash_blocks` off) he gets stuck in forests and villages. Without line of sight he can't bite or breathe, so he just stands at the obstacle.
18. **Floor-layer exception** [test]. The floor layer is kept except for nodes needing a real dig ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.1). Without that exception the chest in `gojira_smash` survived: he had stepped onto the natural-stone ring and crossed the house one node up, with the chest in his floor layer.
19. **Die pose sinks about 0.7 nodes** into the ground at frame 240 [test, model self-check print].
20. **Killed mid-bite or mid-stride (fixed)** [code]: he played `"punch"` instead of `"die"`, or slid through the `die` clip. `kaiju_core.check_for_death` (line 305) handles both, see §3.8. No scenario asserts it.

## 8. Interactions

### Mothra

`kaiju/mothra/init.lua:326-345` (see [`../mothra/README.md`](../mothra/README.md) §3.5):

```lua
local function hunts(self, self_pos, obj, ent) return ent and ent.name == "gojira:gojira" end
local function hunts_ghidorah(self, self_pos, obj, ent) return ent and ent.name == "ghidorah:ghidorah" end
mothra._targeting_rules = {
    mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts_ghidorah, nil, nil, nil), -- 1. Ghidorah in sight
    mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts, nil, nil, nil),       -- 2. Gojira in sight
    mcl_mobs.build_retaliation_target_rule({"mothra:mothra"}, false, nil), -- 3. attackers (never Mothra)
    mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", guards_against, nil, nil, nil), -- 4. any monster
}
```

- Mothra targets Gojira on sight unless a Ghidorah is in sight (view range 64, line of sight required, lost after 3 s out of sight). Her gust and powder damage goes through `mcl_util.deal_damage` with `source = self.object`. That bypasses Gojira's armor and sets his `_last_attacker`, so **his retaliation rule (#2) switches him to her**, even away from a player. A Ghidorah in his sight (#1) still outranks her.
- She circles about 25 nodes up, more than 15° above his mouth, so his fire breath at her is `breath_up` (§3.4). The 9-32 node breath window is measured in 3D from his feet, so she must stay within 32 nodes for him to breathe at her at all. His bite (reach 7) can't touch her at that altitude.
- Gojira's flames hit her through `hit_mob` and credit Gojira as the source. She retaliates (rule 3) but already prefers him (rule 2).
- She is `type = "animal"`, so his iron-golem and player rules never pick her. He fights her only after she attacks him.
- Balance per the last commit: 1 v 1 is roughly even, two Mothras win (`kaiju_fight`).

### Ghidorah

King Ghidorah (`ghidorah:ghidorah`, `type = "monster"`, 600 HP) is documented in
[`../ghidorah/README.md`](../ghidorah/README.md). From Gojira's side:

- **Truce after his death.** When a Ghidorah dies with Gojiras and Mothras within 200 nodes
  (and no other living Ghidorah there), `kaiju_core.start_truce` gives every Gojira a goal
  150 nodes away from the Mothras and every Mothra one 150 nodes the other way
  ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.10). Until he arrives, or for 3
  minutes at most, Gojira has no target (`get_active_target`) and the `retreat` AI function
  walks him there in a straight line at full speed; smashing, wading and swimming work as
  on a bulldoze. Then the normal AI is back. The retreat also ends early when he is
  attacked (by anyone who is not retreating too), when a living Ghidorah comes into view,
  and when he is stuck for 3 s, e.g. in front of a cliff he may not break
  (`tests/kaiju_truce_end`). The two species are then about 300 nodes
  apart, beyond his view range of 58 and hers of 64. In `kaiju_truce` (distance shortened
  to 40) two Gojiras each moved 37 nodes along the common direction with no target.

- Gojira's rule 1 (`init.lua:290`) targets him on sight, ahead of retaliation, and keeps him without line of sight. Mothra's rule 1 does the same (with a 3 s sight limit), so the two team up against him. Gojira's mod only refers to him by the string `"ghidorah:ghidorah"`; without the ghidorah mod the rule never matches.
- Gojira fights him like any target: bite within reach 7, fire breath at 9-32 nodes, `breath_up` when Ghidorah flies more than 15° above his mouth (§3.4).
- Ghidorah's retaliation rule does not alert others, so Gojira's `build_alert_receiver_rule()` (#5) stays unused.

Patterns to reuse for a further kaiju:
- **Targeting:** add a rule to `gojira._targeting_rules` (`init.lua:288-296`) at the priority you want, as the Ghidorah rule does:
  ```lua
  mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", {"ghidorah:ghidorah"}, nil, nil, true), -- esp: keep him without line of sight
  ```

  Add the matching rule to Mothra's list. If a kaiju should rally others, use `broadcast_attack` from its retaliation rule (`build_retaliation_target_rule(ignore, true, {"gojira:gojira"})`). Gojira's `build_alert_receiver_rule()` (#5) will then accept the alert.
- **Fire or lightning breath:** copy the `attack_melee` state machine. Use one `mouth_pos` per head, with the mouth constants printed by its model script at the pose frame. For aerial targets, reuse the `target_above`/`breath_up` split: one pose per direction, chosen once at breath start. Keep `_lifetime` in steps. Set `switch = 1`, `owner_id` and `_shooter`.
- **Model:** use the same conventions (fps 1, 10 u = 1 node, rigid boxes, one action, frame ranges) and print every number Lua needs from the script, as `gojira_model.py` does. Three necks means three `neck*/head*/jaw*` chains under `spine2`.
- **Smashing, wading, bulldozing, knockback:** use `kaiju_core` as Gojira does (`_smash` config, `smash_step` in `ai_step`, `check_standin` with its own swim depth, the three bulldozer calls, `no_knockback`). How to combine them with flight is in [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §5.3. The library sweeps the tail straight behind the mob; a fifth value per segment (a side offset) sweeps it on both sides of that line, which is how Ghidorah's two tails are done (§3.6). A tail at a yaw offset still needs a change in `kaiju_core`'s `sweep_tail`.
- **Size limits:** a wider body makes the 5-node server raycast margin (§7.7) and the smash cost (§7.15) worse. Consider a larger `selectionbox` only together with the raycast limit in mind.
