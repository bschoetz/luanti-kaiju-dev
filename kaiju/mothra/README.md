# Mothra

A giant guardian moth for the [Mineclonia](https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia) game on Luanti.
She flies with her own flight model, ignores players unless they attack her, hunts King Ghidorah first and Gojira second,
and fights monsters with a wing gust and a poison scale powder.

Paths are from the repo root. The mod ships in the `kaiju` modpack, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md).

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `kaiju/mothra/init.lua` | The mob: flight AI, attacks, targeting, registration |
| `kaiju/mothra/mod.conf` | Mod metadata and dependencies |
| `kaiju/kaiju_core/init.lua` | Shared library: `ground_y`, `keep_clearance`, `cruise_y`, `fly_velocity`, `death_fall`, `no_knockback`. See [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md). |
| `kaiju/mothra/models/mothra.glb` | Generated by `tools/mothra_model.py` (Blender, headless) |
| `kaiju/mothra/textures/mothra.png` | Generated by `tools/mothra_texture.py` (PIL) |
| `tests/mothra_show`, `tests/mothra_terrain`, `tests/kaiju_fight`, `tests/kaiju_triangle` | Headless client scenarios, see [`tests/README.md`](../../tests/README.md) |

## 1. Overview

### What she is

- Entity and spawn egg: `mothra:mothra`. `mcl_mobs.register_egg` registers the egg as a craftitem
  with the same name as the entity (`init.lua:351`, egg colours `#e8811a` / `#1e1612`).
- Egg only. No natural spawner is registered, and `_spawn_category = "misc"` keeps her out of the
  monster and animal caps.
- Size: the model is 33.25 units wide (wing tip to wing tip), 18.3 units long (antenna tips to
  hindwing tips) and about 6.8 units tall. At `visual_size` 8 (0.8 nodes per model unit) that is
  **26.6 × 14.6 × 5.4 nodes**. These numbers come from the accessor bounds of a fresh export
  (forewing tips at x = ±16.62 units).
- `persistent = true`, so `despawn_allowed()` is false and she never despawns.

### Attacks

| Attack | Damage | Reach | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wing gust** (`wing_gust`, `init.lua:163`) | 12 (`GUST_DAMAGE`), `type = "mob"`. Knockback through `add_velocity`: 22 n/s horizontally (14 for players) plus 6 n/s up. | Victim's head 0..18 nodes below her feet (`GUST_DEPTH`) and at most 8 above. Horizontally anything within 6 nodes in any direction, otherwise up to 24 nodes (`GUST_RANGE`) inside a cone in front of her (`dot > 0.3`, about ±72°). | Every 4.5 s (`GUST_INTERVAL`). The hit lands 0.65 s after the wind-up (`GUST_HIT_DELAY`). |
| **Scale powder** (`scale_powder`, `init.lua:197`) | Each tick: poison level 2 for 6 s (`give_effect_by_level("poison", obj, 2, 6)`) plus 1 `magic` damage. | Victim's feet 0..30 nodes below her feet (`POWDER_DEPTH`) and ≤ 10 nodes horizontally from her (`POWDER_RADIUS`). | One powder lasts 2 s (`POWDER_TIME`) with a tick every 0.5 s, so 4 to 5 ticks. How poison works (`mcl_potions/functions.lua:213`): level 2 maps to factor 0.6, which means 1 HP every
0.6 s for 6 s. Each powder tick re-applies the effect and refreshes its duration. Poison never takes
the last HP (`get_hp() - 1 > 0`). It does nothing to mobs with `harmed_by_heal` (undead) or with
"spider" in their name. The extra 1 magic damage per tick can kill.

Both attacks require line of sight. `core.line_of_sight` is checked from her **feet** to the top of
the victim's collision box (`visible_top`, `init.lua:53`).

### Temperament

She is a guardian and is registered as `type = "animal"` (`init.lua:75`), so iron golems leave her
alone. Targeting rules (§3.5), in priority order:

1. **King Ghidorah** (`ghidorah:ghidorah`) whenever he is in sight.
2. **Gojira** (`gojira:gojira`) whenever he is in sight.
3. **Retaliation** against whoever damaged her. Another Mothra is never a retaliation target.
4. **Cthulhu**, only while he is after someone other than her or holds a victim.
5. **Any other monster** (`type == "monster"`) in sight.

After a Ghidorah's death she and Gojira part instead of fighting (the truce, §8): for up to 3
minutes she has no target at all, unless she is attacked or another Ghidorah shows up.

She never attacks players unprovoked. A player becomes her target only through retaliation, and
only if mcl_mobs allows attacking that player (`attack_player_allowed`: difficulty is not peaceful,
the player is not in creative mode, and the player is alive).

### Dependencies (`mod.conf`)

- `depends = mcl_mobs, mcl_util, mcl_potions, mobs_mc, kaiju_core`. The first four ship with
  Mineclonia. `mobs_mc` provides the sounds and the smoke particle texture. `kaiju_core` (same
  modpack) provides the flight helpers and the knockback fix.
- `optional_depends = gojira`. This only affects load order. The code refers to Gojira only by the

- `min_minetest_version = 5.10` (needed for glTF animation).
- The drops use `mcl_mobitems` and `mcl_nether` items, which are not declared dependencies. That is
  fine, because the item names are only resolved when she drops them.

Both have `chance = 1` and `looting = "common"`. They drop at the position where she died, which is

## 2. Installation and usage

1. Mothra ships in the `kaiju` modpack, together with Gojira and King Ghidorah. Installing,
   enabling and upgrading the modpack is described in [`../README.md`](../README.md).
2. Her own dependencies are `kaiju_core` (same modpack) and Mineclonia's `mcl_mobs`, `mcl_util`,
   `mcl_potions` and `mobs_mc` (§1). `kaiju_core` is a hard dependency: without it the world
   refuses to load mothra.
3. In game: take the **Mothra** spawn egg from the creative inventory or run
   `/giveme mothra:mothra`, then place it. She spawns on the ground and climbs to cruising height
   (§3.1).

Settings that change her behaviour:

| Setting / gamerule | Effect on Mothra |
|---|---|
| `mcl_difficulty` / `/difficulty` | On peaceful she never targets players, because `attack_player_allowed` is false. |
| Creative mode | Creative players are never valid targets. That is why `tests/kaiju_fight` runs in creative: the observer stays out of the fight. |
| `enable_damage` | Normal Luanti player damage switch |
| `mobs_drop_items` | Enables or disables her drops |
| `mobs_griefing` | No effect on her, since she never changes nodes. It does affect Gojira's smashing and flame fire. |
| `kaiju_smash_blocks`, `kaiju_gojira_fire`, `kaiju_ghidorah_beam_blasts` (modpack settings, `kaiju/settingtypes.txt`) | None applies to her, for the same reason: she breaks no blocks. They are documented in [`../README.md`](../README.md) and [`../gojira/README.md`](../gojira/README.md) §2. |
| `mobs_spawn`, `only_peaceful_mobs` | No effect: she has no natural spawning, and as an `"animal"` she persists in peaceful. |

**On VoxeLibre** (0.92.4, through `kaiju_core/vl.lua`, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md#voxelibre)) she behaves as described here: her flight model,
circling, gust and powder are her own code. Creative players and `only_peaceful_mobs`
protect players there. Fire still burns her; other environmental damage doesn't apply.

## 3. Architecture of `init.lua`

mcl_mobs calls these per server step (`api.lua:542`, `mob_class:on_step`):

```
on_step
  ├─ (dead)  pre_motion_step → motion_step → rotate_step → return
  ├─ navigation_step / movement_step / pre_motion_step
  ├─ motion_step(min(dtime, 0.075), moveresult, attach_pos)   ← overridden (§3.1)
  ├─ ai_step, burning, rotate_step, set_animation_speed …
  ├─ run_targeting_rules(dtime, pos)                          ← her _targeting_rules (§3.5)
  └─ run_ai(dtime, moveresult)                                ← overridden (§3.2)
        └─ check_attack → attack_null                          ← overridden (§3.3)
```

Steering reads `self._goal` and `self._goal_speed`. `run_ai` and `attack_null` write them, and
`motion_step` turns them into velocity on the *next* step.

### 3.1 Flight model (`motion_step`, `init.lua:128-142`)

**What it replaces.** The base `mob_class:motion_step` (`physics.lua:1115`) is mcl_mobs'
Minecraft-style physics: gravity (`fall_speed`), air, water and lava drag, climbing, jumping and
acceleration from `acc_dir`/`acc_speed`, all driven by the engine's collision `moveresult`. None of
it runs for Mothra. Her version:

```lua
function mothra:motion_step(dtime, moveresult, self_pos)
	if self.dead then
		kaiju_core.death_fall(self, self_pos)
		return
	end
	local want = kaiju_core.fly_velocity(self, dtime, self_pos, WANDER_SPEED)
	-- facing (below)
end
```

1. `kaiju_core.fly_velocity` ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6) steers
   the velocity smoothly (time constant about 0.67 s) towards `self._goal` at `_goal_speed`
   (`WANDER_SPEED` 5 or `ATTACK_SPEED` 9, in nodes/s; `WANDER_SPEED` is also the fallback),
   slowing down within `speed / 2` nodes of the goal. **Climb first:** while she is more than
   2 nodes below the clearance floor (`self._floor`, below), she rises on the spot and only then
   crosses higher terrain. The same rule makes her climb straight up after hatching from an egg
   on the ground.
2. Facing (`init.lua:135-141`, her own code): if `self.attack` is valid she uses
   `look_at(target)`. Otherwise she faces the horizontal part of `want`, once that is above
   0.5 n/s. mcl_mobs' `rotate_step` then turns her gradually. `wing_gust` reads
   `object:get_yaw()`, which is this gradually turned yaw.

**`physical = false` (`init.lua:85`), and why.** With a 26-node wingspan and a 5-node collision box
she would snag on every tree, hill and building. Without physics she has no node or object
collisions and no `moveresult` (mcl_mobs substitutes a placeholder), and the engine applies no
gravity. Altitude is therefore kept by choosing goals and by the climb-first rule, not by
collision. The ground is sampled along her whole path (below), so hills and walls make her climb
instead of passing through.

**`ground_y(pos)`** (`init.lua:28` aliases `kaiju_core.ground_y`). A downward raycast that
returns the top of the first node hit, liquids included, so she hovers above lakes. Her callers
start the ray 30 nodes above the relevant height, so a rise of up to about 30 nodes in terrain
is seen. Details: [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6.

**Altitude and clearance.**

- Cruising: `wander` puts each goal at `kaiju_core.cruise_y(goal column, her height, ALTITUDE
  (16) + random(0..6), her body height)`, i.e. 16 to 22 nodes above the ground under the goal,
  or lower under a roof there.
- Fighting: the goal is 11 nodes (`CIRCLE_HEIGHT`) above the target's head, but at most 60 nodes
  (`MAX_ALTITUDE`) above the ground under the target (§3.3).
- Minimum clearance: her local `keep_clearance` (`init.lua:59-61`, called from `run_ai` in both
  branches) calls `kaiju_core.keep_clearance(self, self_pos, dtime, MIN_CLEARANCE)`. Every
  0.25 s it samples the ground along the straight line from her to the goal (up to 13 rays) and
  stores `self._floor = highest ground + MIN_CLEARANCE (10)`; every step it raises `goal.y` to
  at least that floor. Together with climb-first she never cuts through a hill or wall between
  her and the goal.
- Under a roof (a cave, the Nether, a hall: one ray up from her finds it) the same call keeps
  her below it: the path is sampled as columns between floor and roof, the goal stays 2 nodes
  under the lowest roof, and where 10 nodes of clearance don't fit she flies in the middle of
  the gap. A wall, rock or a gap lower than 5.6 nodes (her body + 2) on the path blocks it
  (`self._blocked`): the goal moves to the last point with room. If her own spot is too low she
  moves on towards room without climbing; she never climbs through rock
  ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6).
- In `tests/mothra_terrain`, before path sampling she flew 18 nodes *below* the top of a
  36-high wall, through the stone. With it she climbs first and crosses 10.6 nodes above the
  wall.

**Death fall** (`init.lua:129-132`, `kaiju_core.death_fall`). Once `self.dead` is set, mcl_mobs
keeps calling `motion_step`. On the first dead step she becomes `physical = true` (so she lands
instead of falling through the ground) and gets a fall speed chosen to land within 2.5 s, capped
at 30 n/s ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6). mcl_mobs removes her body
`die frames / die_speed + DEATH_DELAY = 30/10 + 0.5 = 3.5 s` after death (`physics.lua:243`), so
any drop up to 75 nodes lands in about 2.5 s, with 1 s to spare. Before the fix the speed was a
fixed 8 n/s: a Mothra killed at y 58.5 was removed in mid-air at y 30.7. Now she lands at y 8.5
after about 2.6 s (`tests/mothra_terrain` phase C). `check_for_death` already sets
`collide_with_objects = false`. If you change her `die` clip, keep it (plus 0.5 s) longer than
2.5 s.

She keeps the stock `mob_class.check_for_death`. `kaiju_core.check_for_death`, which Gojira and
Ghidorah assign, is not used: it clears a leftover melee punch animation and halts a walking mob
on death. She has neither problem. `attack_type = "null"` never sets mcl_mobs'
`_punch_animation_timeout` (her `"punch"` clip is played through `set_anim`), and her own
`motion_step` replaces her velocity with the death fall from the first dead step on.

### 3.2 `run_ai` (`init.lua:292`), the ghast pattern

**What it replaces.** The base `mob_class:run_ai` (`movement.lua:1459`) loops over
`self.ai_functions` (`check_attack`, `check_pace`, ...) as interruptible "activities" that steer
through the pathfinder (`gopath`, `go_to_pos`, `movement_goal`), and calls
`set_animation("stand")` when nothing is active. Pathfinding is pointless for a non-physical flyer,
so she does what Mineclonia's ghast does and runs a fixed sequence instead:

```lua
_anim_lock -= dtime
if check_attack(self_pos, dtime) then keep_clearance(); return end   -- fighting (kaiju_core)
if self.attacking then self.attacking = nil; reset_attack() end         -- fight just ended
if kaiju_core.retreat_goal(self) then <fly towards it> else wander() end   -- truce (§8)
keep_clearance()
```

`check_attack` (`combat.lua:1244`) is the base version and is not overridden:

- No `self.attack` yet, and the targeting rules produced one: it sets `self.attack`, sets
  `self.attacking = false`, and returns `"attack"` *without* calling `attack_null` on that step.
- Current target invalid or no longer the active target: it clears `self.attack`, calls
  `attack_end()`, and returns `true` (still "fighting" for that step, so nothing is reset).
- Otherwise it calls `self:attack_null(attach_pos, self_pos, dtime, target_pos, line_of_sight)`
  and returns `true`.

**Wander (`init.lua:150`).** A new goal is picked when there is none, when the 6-12 s timer
(`_wander_timer`) runs out, or when she is within 2 nodes of the goal. The goal lies in a random
direction, 16-40 nodes away horizontally, and 16-22 nodes above the ground there (see above). She
flies at `WANDER_SPEED` 5. The animation is `"fly"` while the goal is more than 4 nodes away and
`"stand"` (hover) otherwise. She never lands. Under a roof a blocked goal (`self._blocked`) is
replaced at once by one in another direction, and every new goal is checked by `keep_clearance`
in the same step (`_floor = nil`): in a corner, the moments flown towards goals that had not
been checked yet used to add up to a flight through the wall (`mothra_ceiling`).

**Retreat (truce, §8).** While `kaiju_core.retreat_goal(self)` returns a goal, she flies towards it
instead of wandering: `_goal` is the point 30 nodes ahead on the straight line (or the goal itself
when closer), `ALTITUDE` above the ground there (`cruise_y`: lower under a roof), at
`WANDER_SPEED`. A leg blocked under a roof ends at the last point with room, and she holds
there until the retreat's timeout (180 s). The legs are short so that
`keep_clearance` samples the terrain as densely as on a wander. A leg (one ground raycast) is only
computed when she has none or is within 5 nodes of its end, not every step; `_retreat_leg`
remembers which `_goal` is a leg. `mothra.get_active_target =
kaiju_core.get_active_target` gives her no target during that time, so `check_attack` ends a
running fight and starts none.

### 3.3 `attack_null` (`init.lua:219`): circling, gust and powder

**What it replaces.** The base class has no generic `attack_null`. `check_attack` only calls it if
the mob defines one, which is why `attack_type = "null"` is set. It replaces mcl_mobs' melee,
ranged, bow and crossbow handlers.

**Initialisation.** When `self.attacking` is falsy (set to `false` by `check_attack` on every newly
acquired target, including a direct switch from one target to another), she sets
`attacking = true` and calls `reset_attack()`. That clears all per-fight state: `_gust_hit`,
`_powder_time`, `_powder_approach` and `_powder_tick`, and sets `_anim_lock = 0`. She then picks a
random circle phase `_circle` and starts `_gust_timer = 2` and `_powder_timer = 5`, so the first
gust comes after 2 s and the first powder attempt after 5 s. Before this fix, a direct switch kept
a pending gust or powder alive into the next fight, because `run_ai` only cleared it on an idle
step, and a switch never produces one.

**Circling, every step.**

- `_circle += dtime * ATTACK_SPEED / CIRCLE_RADIUS`. The goal point moves round the circle at
  9 n/s, so one lap takes 2π·15/9 ≈ 10.5 s.
- `goal = target_pos + (cos, sin) * r` horizontally, with
  `goal.y = min(target_pos.y + height_of(target) + CIRCLE_HEIGHT, ground_y(target_pos) + MAX_ALTITUDE)`
  (`init.lua:234`): 11 above the top of the target's collision box, capped at 60 nodes above the
  ground under the target. `target_pos` is the target's attachment position (feet), and
  `height_of` is its `collisionbox[5]`. The cap keeps her from climbing without end over a flying
  Ghidorah. It never matters for a target on the ground (Gojira: 14.5 + 11 = 25.5). For its
  limits see §7.17.
- `r = CIRCLE_RADIUS` (15). During the powder approach and the powder itself,
  `r = POWDER_OFFSET` (5). She dusts from slightly to the side, not right above the target's mouth.
  The first version used `r = 0`.
- **No sight:** if `line_of_sight` is false (mcl_mobs' eye-to-eye `target_visible`, which
  `check_attack` passes in), `r = 0`. She flies straight over the target, at the same height and
  subject to the clearance floor, until she sees it again, then returns to her circle.
- `_goal_speed = ATTACK_SPEED` (9).
- `flat_dist` is the horizontal distance from her to the target.

**State machine.** It is an if/elseif chain evaluated once per step, so only one branch runs:

| Priority | State | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gust pending | `_gust_hit` set | Count down. At ≤ 0, `wing_gust()` runs and the state is cleared. |
| 2 | Powdering | `_powder_time` set | Count down. Every 0.5 s (`_powder_tick`) `scale_powder()` runs. At ≤ 0: clear the state and set `_anim_lock = 0`. |
| 3 | Powder approach | `_powder_approach` set | Count down from 4 s (`POWDER_APPROACH`). If `flat_dist < POWDER_RADIUS − 2` (8): start the powder (`_powder_time = 2`, `_powder_timer = 11`, animation `"powder"` locked for 2 s, sound `shoot_attack`). If the time runs out: give up and set `_powder_timer = 5.5` (half the interval). Otherwise play `"fly"`. |
| 4 | Powder due | `line_of_sight`, `_powder_timer ≤ 0` and `flat_dist < CIRCLE_RADIUS + 4` (19) | Enter the approach (`_powder_approach = 4`). |
| 5 | Gust due | `line_of_sight`, `_gust_timer ≤ 0` and `flat_dist ≤ GUST_RANGE` (24) | `_gust_timer = 4.5`, `_gust_hit = 0.65`, animation `"punch"` locked for 1.2 s, sound `attack` |
| 6 | Otherwise | | Animation `"fly"` |

Both timers keep counting down in every state. A gust that becomes due during a powder fires right
after it. The powder takes priority over the gust when both are due.

**Gust geometry (`wing_gust`).** The candidates are `objects_inside_radius(feet, GUST_RANGE + GUST_DEPTH = 42)`.
The radius is that large because positions are feet, and a tall victim's head can be in reach while
its feet are far away. Each candidate must pass `is_valid_victim` and `visible_top`, and its top must
satisfy `self.y − top.y < GUST_DEPTH` (18) and `top.y − self.y < 8`, i.e. the head is at most
18 nodes below her feet and less than 8 above them. It is hit if its horizontal distance is under 6, or if it is within 24 and
inside the forward cone. A dust particle cone of 120 particles over 0.4 s is always emitted. The
function returns the number of hits, which no caller uses.

**Powder geometry (`scale_powder`).** It emits 60 glowing yellow particles falling at 3-7 n/s. The
candidates are `objects_inside_radius(feet − 15, 25)`. Each must pass `is_valid_victim` and
`visible_top`, have its feet in `[self.y − 30, self.y]`, and be within 10 nodes horizontally of
*her*. The distance is measured from her, not from her target.

**Line of sight.** There are two checks.

- **Targeting:** `attack_null` receives the `line_of_sight` argument (eye to eye). Without it she
  starts no new gust or powder approach, and she moves over the target to get sight back (above).
  If she still cannot see it, the targeting rule drops it (§3.5: after 3 s for rules 1, 2 and 4,
  15 s for retaliation).
- **Hits:** both attacks also test `core.line_of_sight(her feet, victim top)` for each victim.

In `tests/mothra_terrain` phase B, a husk in a walled 3×3 cell, she regains sight and kills it in
about 8 s. Before the fix she circled blind and dropped the target after 3 s, without landing a
hit.

### 3.4 Victim filter (`is_valid_victim`, `init.lua:35`)

- Never herself, never invalid objects.
- Players: only if the player *is* her current `self.attack`. She is a guardian, so a bystander
  standing under a fight is never hit.
- Entities: they must be mcl_mobs mobs (`is_mob`), not dead, not `mothra:mothra`, and either her
  current target or `type == "monster"`.
- Not Gojira while her target is a Ghidorah: he is her ally then.
- Not Cthulhu while he has no target: a stray gust would make him turn on her
  ([`../cthulhu/README.md`](../cthulhu/README.md) §8).

The result is no friendly fire on villagers, animals, pets, golems or other Mothras. Non-mob entities
(items, arrows, boats, carts) are skipped too. Monsters that are *not* her target still take
collateral hits. Any mob she hits records her as `_last_attacker`, so monsters, and Gojira, will
retaliate.

The Gojira exception exists because he fights Ghidorah in melee, 6 nodes from her target and
well inside the gust cone and the powder radius. Until it was added, every gust and every cloud
of powder meant for Ghidorah hit Gojira too, and the push threw him out of melee and into
Ghidorah's beams. In `tests/kaiju_triangle` he died after
60 to 72 s that way, against 105 s when he fought Ghidorah alone
([`../ghidorah/README.md`](../ghidorah/README.md) §8, Balance). Without a Ghidorah as her target
nothing changes: Gojira is her enemy.

### 3.5 Targeting rules (`init.lua:326-345`)

`mob_class:run_targeting_rules` (`combat.lua:13`) runs every step and walks `_targeting_rules` in
order. The first rule that returns a valid object wins and becomes `_active_target`, which
`check_attack` then follows. A rule that is *current* only re-checks its own target
(`track_current_target`). A rule that is *not* current searches at most every 0.5 s (the shared
`"seek_target"` timer). A higher rule therefore pre-empts a lower one within 0.5 s of its condition
becoming true.

| # | Builder | Selects | Acquire | Keep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts_ghidorah)` | nearest `ghidorah:ghidorah` | within `view_range` 64 and `target_visible` (eye to eye) | within `tracking_distance` 80. Dropped after 3 s without sight (`SIGHT_PERSISTENCE`). |
| 2 | `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts)` | nearest `gojira:gojira` | like rule 1 | like rule 1 |
| 3 | `build_retaliation_target_rule({"mothra:mothra"}, false, nil)` | her last attacker (`read_last_attacker`), except other Mothras and her owner. No alerting of others. | attacker within 80 and passing `test_object_and_restriction` (for players this includes `attack_player_allowed`) | within 80. Dropped after 15 s without sight (`RETALIATION_PERSISTENCE`). The current attacker keeps priority over newer ones. |
| 4 | `guards_against_cthulhu` (wraps `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", cthulhu_aggressive)`) | nearest `cthulhu:cthulhu` whose `attack` is someone other than her, or who holds a victim (`_grab_t`) | like rule 1 | like rule 1, and dropped at once when the condition ends: the wrapper asks the predicate again, which the stock rule does not do for a target it has. He is also lost when he dives (his `valid_enemy`). |
| 5 | `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", guards_against)` | nearest `type == "monster"` mob except Cthulhu | like rule 1 | like rule 1 |

**Why this order.**

- Ghidorah comes first, ahead of Gojira: as in the films, Mothra and Gojira team up against him
  (Gojira's first rule targets Ghidorah too). In `tests/kaiju_triangle` both show
  `target=ghidorah` from t = 1. Once Ghidorah is dead the truce (§8) keeps her from turning
  on Gojira at once; she hunts him again when they meet later.
- Gojira comes before retaliation so that a zombie or a skeleton's arrow never distracts her from
  him.
- Retaliation comes before guarding so she answers whoever is actually hurting her (a skeleton, or
  a survival player who hit her) rather than the nearest idle monster.
- Other Mothras are excluded from retaliation. An early version had two Mothras hitting each other
  with the gust, then retaliating against each other instead of fighting Gojira (see the comment in
  the first commit, `58e3b82`). Two layers now prevent this: `is_valid_victim` never hits a Mothra,
  and even an indirect hit does not start a duel.

### 3.6 Animation locking (`set_anim`, `init.lua:144`)

`mob_class:set_animation(name)` (`effects.lua:209`) looks up `animation[name.."_start/_end/_speed/_loop"]`.
It returns early if `name` is already the current animation, so calling it every step does not
restart the clip. It does not, however, stop the AI from replacing a one-shot clip with `"fly"` on
the very next step. `set_anim(self, name, lock)` adds that protection:

- While `_anim_lock > 0`, every `set_anim` call is ignored. `run_ai` decrements the lock every step.
- The gust sets `_anim_lock = 0` (to break any running lock), then calls `set_anim("punch", 1.2)`.
  1.2 s is the full punch clip: 30 frames at 25 fps.
- The powder calls `set_anim("powder", 2.0)`. When the powder ends, it clears the lock (`= 0`) so
  `"fly"` resumes on the next step.
- `wander` and the "otherwise" branch call `set_anim` without a lock, which sets `_anim_lock = nil`.

`"punch"` is non-looping (`punch_loop = false`), so it holds its last frame if nothing replaces it.
`"powder"` loops: four 0.5 s flutter cycles in 2 s.

## 5. Model and texture pipeline

### 5.1 Conventions (shared with `tools/gojira_model.py`)

- Blender is Z-up. The model faces **+Y**: head at +Y, abdomen at −Y. The origin is at her feet.
  The exporter converts to glTF Y-up (`export_yup=True`).
- 10 model units = 1 node at `visual_size` 1. With `visual_size` 8, 1 unit = 0.8 nodes.
- `scene.render.fps = 1`, so glTF time in seconds equals the Blender frame number, which equals the
  frame number in the Lua `animation` table.
- Rigid per-box skinning: every vertex of a box has weight 1.0 on exactly one bone
  (`mothra_model.py:176`). There are no smooth deformations, so boxes rotate as solid pieces about
  their bone's head.
- 256 × 256 atlas. Box faces get UVs from `face_uvs` (§5.5).
- Building blocks: `box(center, size, bone, tile, rot, flip, only)` and `seg(p0, p1, w, h, bone, tile)`
  (a box stretched between two points, extended 0.1 at each end). The result is 33 boxes,
  264 Blender vertices (664 after the glTF UV split) and 18 bones.

### 5.2 Wing geometry (`WINGS`, `mothra_model.py:33`)

Tuple: `(pivot at the root, span, chord, leading-edge offset ahead of the pivot, sweep in degrees
(+ = forwards), tile, thickness)`.

Note: the comment above `WINGS` lists six fields. The tuples have a seventh, the plate thickness,
which is unpacked as `thick` at `mothra_model.py:97`.

| Wing | Pivot (+X side) | Span | Chord | Lead | Sweep | Tile | Thickness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wingf` (fore) | (1.6, 3.0, 2.95) | 14.0 | 7.0 | 0.6 | +12° (forward) | FOREWING 128×64 | 0.08 |
| `wingh` (hind) | (1.5, 0.4, 2.75) | 9.6 | 6.4 | 0.8 | −25° (back) | HINDWING 96×64 | 0.06 |

- Each wing is a thin box that emits only its `top` and `bottom` faces. The box is centred at
  `pivot + R_z(sweep) · (span/2, lead − chord/2, 0)`, so its root edge passes through the pivot and
  its leading edge sits `lead` ahead of it. The −X side is mirrored with `x → −x` and
  `sweep → −sweep`, and its UVs are flipped (`flip=True`).
- The forewing sweeps forwards and the hindwing backwards so they barely overlap from above (the
  re-plan in `eae1d96`). Span:chord equals each tile's aspect (2:1 and 1.5:1), so wing texels come
  out square.
- Wing bones run from the pivot along ±X by `span`, unswept. Since skinning is rigid, only the bone
  head (the rotation pivot) matters.

### 5.4 Pose conventions and the wing roll sign

`key(frame, loc, **rots)` (`mothra_model.py:186`) takes **armature-space** Euler angles in degrees
(XYZ) per bone and converts them with `rest⁻¹ · q · rest`. Every angle is therefore about the
global model axes, pivoting at the bone head, whatever direction the bone points. Bones not listed
in a key get identity. `loc` is also in armature space.

- **X = pitch.** + lifts whatever lies ahead (+Y) of the pivot: nose up for `body`. For a bone that
  points *backwards* (the abdomen) the same + rotation moves its tip **down**. So `abd1..3 = +12..+16`
  in the powder pose curls the abdomen down.
- **Y = roll** about the body's long axis (+Y, forwards). By the right-hand rule, a positive
  rotation about +Y moves +X down (z' = −x·sinθ) and −X up.
- **Z = yaw.** + turns +X towards +Y, which is why positive `sweep` moves the `_l` wingtip forwards.

`wings(fore, hind, sweep)` (`mothra_model.py:198`) hides the sign:

```python
{"wingf_l": (0, -fore, sweep), "wingf_r": (0, fore, -sweep),
 "wingh_l": (0, -hind, sweep*0.5), "wingh_r": (0, hind, -sweep*0.5)}
```

**`fore`/`hind` > 0 means wings up on both sides.** The +X wing (`_l`) needs a *negative* Y roll to
rise, and the −X wing a positive one. The first commit (`58e3b82`) had these signs, and the abdomen
curl, the other way round, so *every* pose was inverted: the hover was wings down, and the gust
raised the wings on the downbeat. `eae1d96` fixed it. When you add poses, call `wings()` rather than
writing roll angles by hand, and check the result in `tests/mothra_show`.

Hindwings use half the sweep. `LEGS_TUCKED` swings every leg −35° about X (backwards and up).

### 5.5 Animations (`mothra_model.py:224-247`)

`flap_cycle(start, length, amp, mid, step, bob, pitch, extra)` keys every `step` frames from
`start` to `start + length` inclusive, with phase `ph = 2π(f − start)/length`:

- `fore = mid + amp·cos(ph)`, `hind = mid + amp·cos(ph − 0.6)` (the hindwings lag)
- `body` pitch `= pitch + 3·sin(ph)`, `abd1/2/3` pitch `−4/−4/−5 · sin(ph − 0/0.5/1.0)` (wave),
  antennae `6·sin(ph)`, legs tucked
- `root` z offset `= −bob·cos(ph)`: lowest with the wings fully up, highest at the bottom of the
  downstroke ("body rises on the downstroke")

| Clip | Frames | Definition | Pose |
|---|---|---|---|
| stand | 0-40 | `flap_cycle(0, 40, amp 22, mid 8, step 2, bob 0.25)` | slow, shallow hover beats |
| fly | 50-80 | `flap_cycle(50, 30, amp 42, mid 5, step 2, bob 0.5, pitch −4)` | full heavy beats, nose 4° down |
| punch | 90-120 | keys at 90, 100, 106, 112, 120 | 90 neutral (wings 5/5). 100 body pitched +35° (upright), wings raised 75/65, swept back −10. **106 downbeat**: wings −55/−50, swept forward +15, body +30. 112 follow-through (−40/−35, body +20). 120 neutral. |
| powder | 130-170 | four `flap_cycle(…, 10, amp 28, mid 15, step 1, bob 0.15, pitch 10)` | fast flutter (0.5 s per beat at 20 fps), nose up 10°, abdomen curled down 12/14/16° |
| die | 180-210 | keys at 180, 195, 210 | Wings fold up (80/70, then 85/80), legs curl (−70, then −80), `root` rolls 60° then 90° about Y and is lifted 1.5, then 2.2 units, because the roll pivots at her feet and she should lie *on* the ground, not in it. |

Adjacent powder cycles share their boundary frames (140, 150, 160). Both write the same values,
since cos and sin are periodic.

- `FIT_TILES` (wings, eye, antenna): the whole tile is stretched onto the face, inset 0.5 px.
- The other tiles use `DENSITY = 12` px per model unit, capped at the tile size and centred, so
  fur stays the same scale on boxes of different sizes.
- `flip` mirrors u for the −X wings. Antenna `top`/`bottom` faces swap u and v so the shaft (image x)
  runs along the plate's length.

### 5.7 Face filtering (`only=...`)

`FACES` names the box faces: `top` +Z, `bottom` −Z, `front` +Y, `back` −Y, `right` +X, `left` −X.
`only=(...)` emits just those faces:

- Wings: `("top", "bottom")`. The 0.06-0.08-unit edges would otherwise show stretched tile pixels.
- Antennae: two crossed plates per side, a flat one (`top`, `bottom`) and an upright one (`left`,
  `right`), so they read from every angle without edge faces.
- Everything else emits all six faces.

`bmesh.ops.recalc_face_normals` orients the normals afterwards. Each plate emits both of its sides,
so it is visible from above and below whatever the backface culling setting.

### 5.8 glTF export (`mothra_model.py:252`)

`GLB`, `export_yup` (Z-up to Y-up), `export_apply`, texcoords, normals, materials exported but
`export_image_format="NONE"` (no embedded image: Luanti uses `textures = {"mothra.png"}`), skins,
animations with `export_animation_mode="ACTIONS"` (one action named `mothra`, frames 0-210),
`export_force_sampling` (baked per frame, i.e. per second at 1 fps), `export_frame_range=False`,
`export_anim_single_armature`, `export_def_bones=False` (all 18 bones become joints), no leaf bones,
no morph targets, extras, cameras or lights.

### 5.9 Regenerating

From the repo root:

```sh
blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/mothra_model.py -- kaiju/mothra/models/mothra.glb
python3 tools/mothra_texture.py kaiju/mothra/textures/mothra.png
```

current script to a scratch path is byte-identical to the committed `mothra.glb`, so a diff in the

pass a path outside it (for example `/tmp/x.glb`) and copy it over once you are happy. Then check it
visually with `tests/run.sh mothra_show 30`.

## 6. Balance (from `tests/kaiju_fight`)

| Setup (60 s logged) | Gojira HP | Mothra HP |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Mothra vs Gojira (the scenario with the second Mothra removed) | 500 → 303 | 350 → 240 |
| 2 Mothras vs Gojira (the committed scenario) | 500 → 153 | |
| 2 Mothras, after the four fixes (one run) | 500 → 131 | 350 → 274 and 350 → 167 |

The commit message summarises it as "1v1 roughly even, two Mothras win". Two Mothras win by far more
than double, because Gojira's retaliation rule tracks one attacker and "Don't be distracted by other attackers"
(`combat.lua:299`). The second Mothra attacks undisturbed.

Rough damage budget against Gojira (upper bounds, not measured):

- Gust: 12 HP per 4.5 s, about 160 HP/min if every gust lands. Gusts don't fire during a powder.
- Powder: 4-5 magic HP + about 10 HP poison, every ≥ 11 s, about 70 HP/min.

Gojira's armor (40) does not reduce either, because both use `deal_damage`.

What Gojira does to her: his melee (`reach` 7 from his centre) cannot reach her. His fire breath
(the `BREATH_*` constants at the top of `kaiju/gojira/init.lua`) fires when the distance from his feet to
her feet is 9..32 nodes, he has line of sight, and his 7 s cooldown is over. Each flame hits for 2
(`deal_damage`, ignores her armor) and sets her on fire for 6 s. While she circles she is
√(15² + (14.5 + 11)²) ≈ **29.6** nodes away, and about 26 while dusting: inside his range.

He uses a `breath_up` pose (frames 250-300, mouth higher and further back) whenever the target's
centre is more than 15° (`BREATH_UP_ANGLE`) above his mouth. Against a circling Mothra that is
always the case, so `tests/kaiju_fight` logs `anim=breath_up`.

| Goal | Turn |
|---|---|
| Her damage output | `GUST_DAMAGE`, `GUST_INTERVAL`, `POWDER_INTERVAL`, poison level and duration and the per-tick magic damage (`init.lua:212-213`) |
| Her survivability | `hp_max`. `CIRCLE_RADIUS`/`CIRCLE_HEIGHT` relative to Gojira's `BREATH_MAX_DIST` 32. With `CIRCLE_HEIGHT` ≥ 14 (at radius 15) she circles out of breath range entirely and becomes nearly untouchable by him. That is a cliff, not a slider. |
| Gojira's side | `BREATH_COOLDOWN`, `BREATH_MAX_DIST`, flame damage (`get_arrow_damage_func(2, …)`), his `hp_max` |

After changing any of these, re-run `tests/run.sh kaiju_fight` and compare the HP columns at t = 60.
For a 1v1 run, remove the `m2` spawn in `tests/kaiju_fight/init.lua` locally.

## 7. Known limitations and gotchas

"Verified" means the claim follows from reading the code. "Unverified" means it was not exercised in
a test.

1. **She never lands** (verified). There is no landing or perching code. The only time she touches
   the ground is when she falls after death.
2. **The clearance clamp has only been tested on flat worlds** (unverified elsewhere). All scenarios
   use `mg_name = flat`.
3. **Fixed: flying through terrain.** `kaiju_core.keep_clearance` samples the whole path and she
   climbs first (§3.1), as tested with a 36-high wall in `tests/mothra_terrain`. Still open: only
   the line under her centre is sampled, so her 26-node wings can clip a tower or a cave wall
   beside the path, and under the open sky a 1-2 node wide spike between samples can be missed
   ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.9).
4. **Fixed: ceilings** (verified, `tests/mothra_ceiling`; one run in a real Nether, see
   `tests/README.md`). She used to climb into any roof within 30 nodes above her. Now she stays
   below it (§3.1). What she does not do: look for a way around. A blocked wander goal is
   replaced; in a fight or on a retreat she flies as far as there is room and holds there, so a
   target behind rock or on top of the overhang she is under stays out of reach until it moves
   or the rule drops it. A cave narrower than her wings does not stop her: the wings clip the
   walls (limitation 3). Spawned in a tunnel lower than 5.6 nodes she moves towards room if
   there is any on her path, else she stays in the middle of the gap.
5. **Line of sight was not tested through roofs** (unverified). Both attacks check
   `core.line_of_sight(her feet, victim top)`, so a roof should protect a victim, but no scenario
   covers it. Targeting uses mcl_mobs' own eye-to-eye check (feet + 2.6). The attacks use her feet.
   The two can disagree right at the edge of cover.
6. **The front view is dominated by the ruff** (verified in the geometry, visual judgement). The
   white ruff box (4.6 × 4.2 units) is wider and taller than the head (2.8 × 2.4) and sits just
   behind it, so head-on she reads as a white disc with eyes. Side and top views show the wings.
7. **Fixed: circling a target she cannot see.** Without sight she flies over the target and starts
   no new attack (§3.3). If the target is under a roof she still cannot see it from above, and the
   rule drops it after 3 s, or 15 s for retaliation.
8. **Poison does nothing to undead and spiders** (verified, `mcl_potions` `res_condition`). Against
   zombies and skeletons the powder only deals its 1 HP/tick magic damage. Poison never kills by
   itself.
9. **Armor only affects punches** (verified). `armor = 60` reduces player and melee hits, not
   flames, powder or other `deal_damage` sources.
10. **Knockback ignores `knockback_resistance`** (verified). The gust uses `obj:add_velocity`, so
    even Gojira (resistance 1.0) is shoved 22 n/s until mcl_mobs friction brakes him.
11. **Fixed: state surviving a target switch.** `reset_attack()` runs at the start of every fight
    (§3.3).
12. **Fixed: vanishing in mid-air after death.** `kaiju_core.death_fall` picks a fall speed to land
    in 2.5 s (§3.1). That works up to about 75 nodes, and above that at the 30 n/s cap (drops above
    105 nodes still vanish). The drops spawn at the point of death and fall on their own
    ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.13).
13. **Peaceful and creative** (verified). Players in creative or on peaceful can never become her
    target, even by hitting her. To test her against a player, use survival and difficulty ≥ easy.
14. **Ground includes water and anything pointable** (water verified via `liquids = true`; plants
    and leaves follow from raycast pointability, unverified; see
    [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §6.11). Trees raise her clearance, which is
    intended.
15. **`WINGS` comment** (verified). `WINGS` has one more field than its comment (§5.2).
16. **Punches don't stop her** (verified in code). `kaiju_core.no_knockback(mothra)` turns off
    mcl_mobs' punch and projectile knockback, which with `knockback_resistance = 1.0` would set
    her velocity to zero ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md)).
17. **`MAX_ALTITUDE` and high flyers** (verified in code, not exercised in a test). With the cap
    she stays at most 60 nodes above the ground under her target. The gust only hits a victim
    whose top is less than 8 nodes above her feet, and the powder only victims whose feet are
    below hers. A Ghidorah (15 nodes tall) flying with his feet more than about 53 nodes above
    the ground is therefore out of her gust, and above 60 also out of her powder.
    `kaiju_core.ground_y` scans only 64 nodes down: over a deeper drop it returns
    `target_pos.y − 64`, and the cap puts her 4 nodes *below* the target's feet.

## 8. Gojira and Ghidorah

### With Gojira

- She prefers him (rule 2) whenever he is within 64 nodes and visible and no Ghidorah is, and
  keeps him up to 80 nodes away.
- She circles 11 nodes above his 14.5-node collision box, about 25.5 above his feet, at a radius of
  15. Every gust and powder records her as his `_last_attacker`. His retaliation rule
  (`gojira._targeting_rules`, second after his own Ghidorah rule) ranks above players, so he
  switches from players to her and answers with fire
  breath only, aimed upwards with `breath_up` (§6). His bulldozing and tail sweep act on nodes and never reach her.
- With two Mothras he retaliates against one, and the other attacks freely.
- She never hits Gojira by accident while guarding something else: he is a monster, so he is a valid
  victim anyway, and he is also her second-priority target. The one exception is a fight
  against Ghidorah, where she spares him (§3.4).
- **Truce.** When a Ghidorah dies within 200 nodes of them, `kaiju_core.start_truce` sends every
  Gojira 150 nodes away from the Mothras and every Mothra 150 nodes the other way
  ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.10). She flies there at cruise altitude
  and speed with no target (§3.2); it takes about 30 s. On arrival, or after 3 minutes, the rules
  above apply again (also at once when she is hit by anyone but a truce partner, or when a living
  Ghidorah comes into view: `tests/kaiju_truce_end`), but the species are then about 300 nodes apart, far beyond her view range.
  `tests/kaiju_truce` shows it with the distance shortened to 40.
- `optional_depends = gojira` only orders loading. Neither mod calls the other's code; both call
  `kaiju_core`.

### With Ghidorah (a three-headed dragon that flies and walks)

King Ghidorah (`ghidorah:ghidorah`, `type = "monster"`) is documented in
[`../ghidorah/README.md`](../ghidorah/README.md). The notes below are what matters from her side
and what a further kaiju can reuse.

**Reusing her flight model.** `ground_y`, path clearance (`keep_clearance`), goal steering with
the climb-first rule (`fly_velocity`) and the death fall (`death_fall`) are in `kaiju_core`;
[`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §5.3 shows how a kaiju that walks and flies
switches between them and the ground mechanics. Still local to `kaiju/mothra/init.lua`, and worth
copying:

- the `run_ai` skeleton: `check_attack`, then `keep_clearance`, else wander, plus `reset_attack`
  for per-fight state
- facing in `motion_step` (`look_at` the target, else the flight direction)
- `set_anim` with `_anim_lock`

A kaiju that also walks needs a real `walk` clip (Mothra aliases `walk` to `fly`).

**How Mothra fights him.** `mothra._targeting_rules` (`init.lua:339-345`) starts with a separate
rule for him, *before* the Gojira rule, so he outranks Gojira:

```lua
local function hunts_ghidorah(self, self_pos, obj, ent)
	return ent and ent.name == "ghidorah:ghidorah"
end
mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts_ghidorah, nil, nil, nil),
```

Putting both names into one predicate would instead give them the same priority, and she would
take the nearest. Ghidorah is `type = "monster"`, so rule 4 and `is_valid_victim` cover him too:
he takes collateral hits even while he is not her current target.

**Circling a flyer.** Her circle height is `target_pos.y + height_of(target) + CIRCLE_HEIGHT`, where
`target_pos` is his feet (attachment position) and `height_of` is `collisionbox[5]`. She automatically
circles 11 nodes above an airborne Ghidorah's top, clamped to at least `MIN_CLEARANCE` above the
ground and to at most `MAX_ALTITUDE` (60) above the ground under him (§7.17). Points to check
for any flying target:

- Her `ATTACK_SPEED` (9) and steering lag decide whether she keeps up with a fast climber.
- The gust reaches 18 nodes down and 8 up (from her feet to his top). The powder only hits victims
  whose feet are *below* her and within 30 nodes. If a collision box is taller than 19 nodes,
  `CIRCLE_HEIGHT + height > POWDER_DEPTH` and the powder never reaches it (Ghidorah's is 15).
- For a ranged attack with a range like Gojira's breath (Ghidorah's beams), the coupling to
  `CIRCLE_RADIUS`/`CIRCLE_HEIGHT` in §6 applies again.
- Give a new kaiju's targeting rules the same care: ignore his own kind in retaliation and keep friendly fire
  off, or two Ghidorahs will duel just as the two Mothras once did.
