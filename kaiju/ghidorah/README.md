# King Ghidorah

A giant three-headed golden dragon for [Mineclonia](https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia).
He stands about 20 nodes tall, walks **and** flies, fires a gravity beam from each
head, bites, and smashes through whatever stands in his way. Every head has its own
hit points: a head that loses them is severed and grows back after 30 s. He dies when
his body runs out of HP or when all three heads are off at the same time. He hunts
Gojira, Mothra and players.

This document is for developers who want to change or extend the mob. It describes
what the code does. Where a code comment or the review notes say something different,
the code is documented and the difference is flagged with **Note:**.

Paths below are relative to the repo root.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `kaiju/ghidorah/init.lua` | Mob definition, walk/fly state machine, beams, heads, hit boxes |
| `kaiju/ghidorah/mod.conf` | Mod metadata and dependencies |
| `kaiju/kaiju_core/init.lua` | Shared library: smashing, tail sweep, bulldozer, wading, flight helpers, knockback and death fixes. See [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md). |

## 2. Installation and settings

Installation is described in the modpack README, [`../README.md`](../README.md):
install the one `kaiju` folder and enable the `kaiju` modpack. `ghidorah` needs
`kaiju_core` from the same modpack and a Mineclonia or VoxeLibre world.

Spawn him with the egg item `ghidorah:ghidorah` (`/giveme ghidorah:ghidorah`, or the
creative inventory). Leave about 20 nodes of headroom.

| Setting | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `mobs_griefing` | `/gamerule mobs_griefing false`, or the per-mob list `mcl_mobs_griefing_disable_individual = ghidorah:ghidorah` | When false for him, he destroys no blocks at all, whatever the two settings below say. |
| `kaiju_smash_blocks` | modpack setting (`../settingtypes.txt`), default `true` | When false, he doesn't smash blocks while walking (`kaiju_core.smash_step`), his tails sweep nothing, and he doesn't clear the spot he lands on (`touch_down`). |
| `kaiju_ghidorah_beam_blasts` | modpack setting, default `true` | When false, the beams leave no craters. |
| Protection | any protection mod | Smashing skips protected nodes (`kaiju_core`). The beam blasts go through `mcl_explosions.explode`. |
| Difficulty, creative mode | | He ignores players on peaceful and creative players (`attack_player_allowed`). The beams pass through such players. |

Both modpack settings are read live on every use through
`kaiju_core.griefing_enabled("ghidorah:ghidorah", <setting>)`, which is true only if
the setting **and** `mobs_mc.is_mob_griefing_enabled` are true. They only cover block
destruction. Beam damage to mobs and players, the bite and his movement stay the same.
Without smashing he is a path-finding mob with a 5.2-node body and gets stuck in
forests.

**On VoxeLibre** (0.92.4, through `kaiju_core/vl.lua`, see
[`../README.md`](../README.md#voxelibre)) he walks, flies, beams, bites, loses and regrows
heads as described here. On foot he walks straight at his target (no pathfinder);
`mobs_griefing` is a plain setting there; his hurt sound is the parrot's (VoxeLibre's
ghast has none).

### 3.2 Per-step flow

`run_ai` runs every step for a living mob, in this order:

1. Count down `_anim_lock` and `_beam_cooldown`.
2. `kaiju_core.ensure_hitboxes`: (re)create the four hit box objects if one is missing.
3. `regrow_step`: count down severed heads.
4. `beams_step`: run the current volley.
5. `choose_mode`: once a second, decide between walking and flying (§3.4).
6. Every 0.2 s: `aim_head` for each head at `head_target` (§3.7).
7. Then, by mode:
   - **On the ground:** stock `mob_class.run_ai`, which runs `ai_functions`
     (`check_attack`, `check_pace`). During the "land" clip he stands still instead.
   - **Landing** (`_land`): play "hover", nothing else. He does not attack.
   - **Flying:** `check_attack`; without a target `fly_wander` and
     `kaiju_core.keep_clearance`.

`attack_type = "null"`, so `check_attack` calls `ghidorah:attack_null`, which
dispatches to `air_attack` or `ground_attack`. A new target shortens the beam cooldown
to at most 2 s.

`ai_step` adds `kaiju_core.smash_step` while he is on the ground. `motion_step` is the
stock one on the ground and `kaiju_core.fly_velocity` in the air.

### 3.3 Targeting rules (in priority order)

```lua
ghidorah._targeting_rules = {
	mcl_mobs.build_retaliation_target_rule({"ghidorah:ghidorah"}, false, nil), -- 1. attackers (never another Ghidorah)
	mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("mob", hunts, nil, nil, true),          -- 2. Gojira or Mothra
	kc.cthulhu_rule(),                                                         -- 3. Cthulhu, within 32 nodes
	mcl_mobs.build_nearest_target_rule("player", nil, nil, nil, true),         -- 4. players
}
```

`hunts` accepts `gojira:gojira` and `mothra:mothra` (`is_archenemy`). Cthulhu is only a
target within 32 nodes and while he is not fully under water
([`../cthulhu/README.md`](../cthulhu/README.md) §8): `kaiju_core.cthulhu_rule`, the rule
Gojira uses too. The side heads' own targets (§3.7) are players and
`kaiju_core.is_kaiju`: those two and Cthulhu (other Ghidorahs are excluded by `is_enemy`). The last argument (`esp`) keeps a
target without line of sight. `self.attack` is the body's target and the centre head's
target; the side heads can pick their own (§3.7).

### 3.4 Walking and flying

One flag, `_flying`, switches the physics, as sketched in
[`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §5.3.

`choose_mode` runs once a second, but not during an animation lock or a beam volley.

| State | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| On the ground, has a target | target more than 6 nodes above its ground (`AIRBORNE_TARGET`), **or** more than 48 nodes away (`FAR_TARGET`), **or** a bulldoze is running (`_bulldoze_time > 0`) and he has flown fewer than 3 times for that reason (`_stuck_flights`); **and** no roof within 6 nodes above his head (`FLY_ROOM`; `kaiju_core.ceiling_y`, leaves are no roof), **and** `_no_fly` has run out | `take_off` |
| Flying, not landing | `kaiju_core.keep_clearance` found the path blocked (`_blocked`: a wall, rock, or a gap lower than 17 nodes under a roof) | `_land = true`, `_no_fly = 20` s (`NO_FLY_TIME`) |
| Flying, has a target | target not airborne and horizontally closer than 25 nodes (`LAND_DIST`) | `_land = true` |
| Flying, no target | 15 checks in a row without a target (`IDLE_FLIGHT`, about 15 s) | `_land = true` |

He never takes off without a target. The gap between 25 and 48 nodes is the
hysteresis: in it he stays in the mode he is in. `_stuck_flights` is reset in
`attack_end`. `_no_fly` (seconds, counted down every step) keeps him on foot after a
blocked flight: without it he took off again at once for the same far target. Under a
roof lower than 21 nodes he stays on foot altogether: he walks, bulldozes and beams.

- **`take_off`:** a running bulldoze is ended (`kaiju_core.end_bulldoze`, so one stuck
  event causes one flight), `physical = false`, navigation cancelled, goal 18 nodes (`ALTITUDE`)
  straight up at 9 nodes/s, or lower: his head stays 2 nodes under a roof. "takeoff" clip
  locked for 1 s, `war_cry` sound. If the way to the target is blocked under that roof, the
  first clearance check after the clip says so and he comes down again after a hop of a
  node or two (`ghidorah_ceiling`, phase "cavern").
- **Landing:** when `_land` is set, `choose_mode` picks the spot (`find_landing_spot`, at
  once for the first three candidates, three more per second): the column below him, then
  the nearest of 8 points on a ring of 12 nodes (`LAND_RING`) around the target (without a
  target within 30 nodes: 10 and 20 nodes around himself). A spot must pass
  `kaiju_core.landing_ok`: level, walkable ground under the centre and the corners of his
  box, no leaves and no liquid on the way down. If none of the 9 does, he comes down
  where he is, as he always did (so with smashing on `touch_down` clears a forest spot,
  and water is the last resort). `_land_spot` is a plain table (it is saved with him).
  `motion_step` hovers until the spot is chosen, flies over to it at his height with
  `keep_clearance` (for at most 10 s, `LAND_TRANSIT`, then he comes down where he is), and
  then sets the goal to the ground directly below him at `FLY_SPEED` and clears `_floor`,
  without `keep_clearance`, so nothing holds him up. Less than 1.5 nodes above the ground
  `touch_down` runs. On open flat ground the first candidate passes and nothing changed.
  Measured before and after the first review: 9 s from 40 nodes at the old 5 nodes/s, 5 s now.
- **`touch_down`:** if `kaiju_core.griefing_enabled(..., "kaiju_smash_blocks")`, one
  `kaiju_core.smash(self, {lookahead = 0})` pass clears his body box, so he doesn't
  land inside trees or houses. Then `physical = true`, velocity zero, "land" clip
  locked for 1 s. The AI pauses until the clip is through.
- **Flight model:** `kaiju_core.fly_velocity` and `keep_clearance` with
  `MIN_CLEARANCE = 12`, see [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6. He
  faces his target, or the flight direction without one.
- **`fly_wander`** (flying without a target): a new goal 20-50 nodes away in a random
  direction, 18-26 nodes above the ground there (`kaiju_core.cruise_y`: lower under a roof),
  every 8-14 s or on arrival, at 5 nodes/s, with the "fly" clip.
- **Death in the air:** `kaiju_core.death_fall`. His die clip plus the delay is 4.5 s,
  longer than the 2.5 s fall.

### 3.5 Ground attack (`ground_attack`)

1. During a full volley he stands and keeps facing the target. Nothing else happens.
2. If no volley is running and the cooldown is over:
   - main target in line of sight and 8 to 40 nodes away (`BEAM_MIN_DIST`,
     `BEAM_RANGE`, measured from his feet), and it has been at least 8 nodes away for
     1.5 s (`BEAM_MIN_TIME`, counted in `_beam_far`): stop, cancel navigation, start a
     **full volley**. The wait is for a melee opponent that a gust of Mothra's or a
     landing has knocked a few nodes away: the two walk back together within a second,
     and he used to fire a whole volley at point-blank range each time. A new target
     starts with the wait already served (`attack_null`), so the opening volley comes
     as before;
   - main target not in that range (too close, too far or out of sight), and a side
     head has a target of its own: start a **side-only volley** and carry on with
     step 3.
3. `kaiju_core.update_bulldoze`, then stock `mob_class.attack_melee`: path to the
   target and bite. `gopath` and `attack_end` are wired to the bulldozer as for Gojira
   ([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.3). The same two calls hold the
   **melee standoff**: he stops when his body is 2 nodes from the target's (7 nodes
   centre to centre against Gojira) instead of walking to its feet. He is `pushable`
   (the mcl_mobs default), so that he and a Gojira who are inside each other anyway, for
   instance after he landed on him, push each other apart. Until this was added the two
   stood inside each other for the whole fight (`kaiju_clinch`).

### 3.6 Air attack (`air_attack`)

He circles the target at radius 20 (`CIRCLE_RADIUS`) and 9 nodes/s, about 14 s per
lap. The circle height is

- for a ground target: the top of its collision box + 10 (`CIRCLE_HEIGHT`);
- for an airborne target: its centre − 7.5, which puts the middle of his body level
  with it;

capped at 40 nodes above the ground below the target (`MAX_ALTITUDE`), and raised by
`keep_clearance`. Without line of sight the radius is 0: he flies straight at the
target to regain sight.

With line of sight, the cooldown over and the target within 40 nodes, he starts a full
volley. There is no minimum distance in the air and he keeps circling while he fires.
He does not bite in the air.

### 3.7 Gravity beams

**Volley** (`start_beams`, `beams_step`, `end_beams`). A volley lasts `BEAM_TIME` =
2.5 s. For the first 0.5 s (`BEAM_START`) he rears up. After that there is a tick
every 0.25 s (`BEAM_TICK`), about 8 per volley. In each tick every living head with a
valid target fires once (`beam_tick`).

| | Full volley | Side-only volley |
|---|---|---|
| Starts when | main target in beam range (§3.5, §3.6) | main target outside the 8-40 node window or out of sight, and a side head has its own target; on the ground only |
| Heads that fire | all living heads | side heads only, and only at a target other than the main one |
| Animation | "beam" on the ground, "beam_fly" in the air, locked for 2.5 s | none: the body keeps walking and biting, the side jaws are opened by a bone override (`set_side_jaws`, 0.7 rad) |
| Movement | stands still on the ground, circles in the air | unchanged |

After a volley the cooldown is 8 s (`BEAM_INTERVAL`) if at least one beam fired, and
2 s if none did (a side head whose enemy got away tries again soon). `attack_end` ends
a running volley.

**Main side volley** (`_beam_side == "main"`, `ground_attack`). With the main target in
melee (closer than `BEAM_MIN_DIST`) and no enemy of their own, the side heads fire a
volley at the main target every `SIDE_MAIN_INTERVAL` (12 s): `SIDE_MAIN_DAMAGE` 1 per head
and tick, and he doesn't bite meanwhile (`pre_melee_attack`). It has its own cooldown
(`_side_main_cd`) and leaves `_beam_cooldown` alone. The gate is `beam_reaches`, a dry run
of the beam: in melee a side head can't turn far enough down to aim at the target's
centre (about 60° yaw and pitch against Gojira), but the clamped beam still enters a
kaiju's box, so it fires; a player below the heads it would miss, so no volley starts
(looked at once a second, and again every tick of the volley). `ghidorah_sidemain`: 3
volleys in 40 s of melee with Gojira, 16 damage each; none at a player 5 nodes in front.

**What a beam hits** (`beam_tick`). Every enemy on the line takes the damage; the beam
ends at its target, at the first player or kaiju, or at a node. Smaller mobs let it
through. Players take `BEAM_PLAYER_DAMAGE` 1 per head and tick instead of 3, and
`beams_step` deals the hits of all heads on one player in a tick as a single hit: armour
takes a share of every hit and VoxeLibre rounds the rest to whole HP, so three hits of 1
would do nothing through iron armour. A full volley (`ghidorah_beam`): 24 on a bare
player, 10 (Mineclonia) or 8 (VoxeLibre) through full iron.

**One beam** (`beam_tick`):

1. The head's joint position comes from `HEAD_JOINT` for the pose of the current
   animation (§3.8).
2. Yaw and pitch from the joint to the centre of the target's collision box, each
   clamped to ±60° (`HEAD_AIM_MAX`) relative to the body. This is the same direction
   `aim_head` turns the head to.
3. The beam starts at `joint + direction * MOUTH_LEN` (4.35 nodes for the centre head,
   3.75 for the side heads) and runs 40 nodes along that direction. A target outside
   the cone is missed.
4. `core.raycast` with objects, without liquids. The first **enemy** on the ray takes
   3 damage through `mcl_util.deal_damage` (`type = "mob"`, source = Ghidorah) and
   stops the beam. An enemy is any living mob that is not a Ghidorah, or a player whom
   `attack_player_allowed` accepts. It does not have to be the head's target. Other
   objects are passed through, including his own hit boxes.
5. The first node on the ray stops the beam. If this tick is a blast tick and
   `kaiju_core.griefing_enabled("ghidorah:ghidorah", "kaiju_ghidorah_beam_blasts")`,
   `mcl_explosions.explode(pointed.above, 1, {drop_chance = 0.1, fire = false}, self.object)`
   goes off there. Blast ticks come once per second of firing (`BLAST_INTERVAL`), for
   all heads in the same tick.
6. Visuals: a particle spawner at the mouth shoots `20 + ceil(4 * distance)` sparks
   per tick at `distance / 0.25 s`, so they reach the hit point as they expire and form
   a continuous line.

**Note:** the comment on `BLAST_INTERVAL` says "per head". There is one timer for all
heads; the effect is the same, since each head that hits a node blasts in that tick.

**Head targets** (`head_target`):

- The centre head always takes `self.attack`.
- A side head keeps its own enemy in `_head_target_l` / `_head_target_r` as long as it
  is valid, an enemy, within 40 nodes and inside the head's aim cone (`can_aim`).
- Otherwise it searches, at most once per game second and head, all objects within 40
  nodes of its joint for a **player or kaiju** that is an enemy, is not the main
  target, passes mcl_mobs' own target checks, is inside the aim cone and in line of
  sight. The nearest one wins. An enemy the other side head already has counts as 40
  nodes further away, so two enemies are split between the heads and a single one gets
  both.
- With no enemy of its own a side head falls back to `self.attack`.
- A severed head has no target: `sever_head` clears `_head_target_X` and `head_target`
  returns nil for it, so a stump starts no side volley.

**Aiming** (`aim_head`, every 0.2 s): a relative rotation override on `head_c`,
`head_l`, `head_r`, `vector.new(-pitch, 0, -yaw_off)` with 0.2 s interpolation. It is
only sent when an angle changed by more than 0.03 rad, so at most 15 messages a second.
During the "beam" clip the clip's own pitch of −14.5° (`BEAM_POSE_PITCH`) is
subtracted, because the override is applied on top of the animation. Without a target
the override is zero.

### 3.8 Heads: positions, hit points, severing, regrowth

**Positions.** The server doesn't know the animated pose. `HEAD_JOINT` holds the pivot
of each `head_X` bone as `{forward, side, up}` in model units for three poses, and
`POSE` maps the current animation to one of them:

| Pose | Animations | Centre head joint (nodes: forward / up) |
|---|---|---|
| `stand` | stand, walk, punch, die, anything not listed | 3.8 / 17.0 |
| `reared` | beam, beam_fly, hover, takeoff, land | 6.2 / 15.2 |
| `fly` | fly | 11.0 / 9.4 |

The side heads sit about 4.5 nodes to either side. `head_joint(self, h, ahead)` turns
the entry into a world position. The skull centre is `HEAD_CENTRE` = 3 units (2.25
nodes) ahead of the joint along the body's forward direction; hit boxes, hit tests and
particles use it.

**Hit points.** `_head_hp = {c, l, r}`, 120 each. `ghidorah:receive_damage` decides
where a hit goes:

- `_hit_part` is set: the hit came through a hit box (§3.9).
- Otherwise `hit_head`: only for a direct hit by another **mob**. The head closest to
  the attacker's body, taken as the vertical line from its feet to the top of its
  collision box, and within 2 nodes (`HEAD_HIT_RADIUS`) of it. In the stand pose his
  heads are 16 to 17 nodes up, and a Gojira is 14.5 tall: at the melee standoff he
  stands below the centre head, 2.5 nodes short of it, and bites the body. He reaches a
  head that comes down to him: one lowered for the beams (`reared` pose, 15.2 up), or
  any head when Ghidorah lands on him. With the radius at 3.5, as it was until the
  standoff put Gojira under that head, every bite went into the centre head until it
  was severed, 14 bites of every 34 (it grows back after 30 s).
- If that names a living head, the damage comes off the head only. He plays the hurt
  sound and a spark burst at the head, and `mob_class.receive_damage(self, reason, 0)`
  keeps mcl_mobs' attacker bookkeeping (retaliation) without touching the body.
- Everything else, including the chest box and a hit on a severed head's box, damages
  the body as usual.

Damage beyond a head's remaining HP is lost; it is not carried over to the body. A
wounded head does not heal. Only regrowth resets it to 120.

**Severing** (`sever_head`): the head's regrow timer is set to `_regrow_time` (30 s,
a per-entity field so tests can shorten it), its own target is dropped, and the bone
`head_X` is scaled to 0.01 over 0.3 s (0 would collapse the bone matrix). The jaw is a
child bone and shrinks with it. What stays visible is the end of `neck_X4`, which is
textured red. A severed head neither aims nor fires. If no head is alive after that,
`self.health = 0` and `check_for_death` runs: **all three heads off at the same time
kill him**, whatever the body HP.

**Regrowth** (`regrow_step`): after the timer the head HP is back at 120, the scale
goes back to 1 over 3 s, and he plays the `random` sound. The head can fire as soon as
the timer is over, while it is still growing. The head's aim cache (`_aim_X`) is cleared,
so `aim_head` sends its rotation again at once (the scale override dropped it).

`ghidorah.check_for_death = kaiju_core.check_for_death` plays "die" instead of a
leftover bite and stops him where he dies.

**After his death** (`ghidorah:on_die`): `kaiju_core.start_truce(self.object)`. The
Gojiras and Mothras within 200 nodes move 150 nodes apart in opposite directions and
attack nobody on the way, unless another living Ghidorah is within that radius
([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.10, scenario `kaiju_truce`).

### 3.9 Hit boxes (`kaiju_core:hitbox`)

The server's raycast only finds objects whose **position** is within 5 nodes of the
ray, and his position is at his feet. Arrows aimed at anything above his knees flew
through him, and one selection box can't follow three heads. So he carries four
invisible objects. The mechanism is `kaiju_core.ensure_hitboxes`
([`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.9; it was `ghidorah:hitbox` in this
file before Gojira got hit boxes too); his part list is `HITBOXES`:

| Part | Position | Selection box |
|---|---|---|
| `c`, `l`, `r` | the skull centre of that head (§3.8) | `{-2, -2, -2, 2, 2, 2}` |
| `chest` | halfway between the hips and the centre neck joint: 1.9 nodes forward and 12.2 up when standing, 5.5 forward and 8.4 up in flight | `{-3, -4.5, -3, 3, 4.5, 3}` |

- The entity is a blank sprite, `physical = false`, `static_save = false`, armor group
  `immortal`, `_hittable_by_projectile = true` (for `mcl_bows`).
- `on_step` moves it to its part every server step and removes it when the owner is
  gone or dead.
- `on_punch` and `deal_damage` (called by `mcl_util.deal_damage`, i.e. projectiles)
  pass the hit on to the owner with `owner._hit_part` set for the duration of the
  call. `receive_damage` reads it.
- `kaiju_core.ensure_hitboxes` keeps the object references in `_hitbox_c`, `_hitbox_l`,
  `_hitbox_r`, `_hitbox_chest`. They are top-level fields because mcl_mobs drops
  ObjectRefs from staticdata only at the top level. After a reload the boxes are
  created again.

His own selection box (`{-3, 0, -3, 3, 15, 3}`) still takes punches to the body.

### 3.10 Smashing, tails, water

All of it is `kaiju_core`, see [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.2-3.4.

- `ghidorah._smash = {lookahead = 3, tail = {segments = TAIL, unit = UNIT, first = 2}}`.
  In the air `ai_step` passes `FLY_SMASH = {lookahead = 12, keep = "natural"}` to
  `smash_step` instead: he tears down what he flies through (trees, buildings), natural
  terrain stays, and his `keep_clearance` calls ignore what he may break, so he flies
  through a tower instead of over it (`kaiju_core/README.md` §3.2, §3.6). With
  `kaiju_smash_blocks` or griefing off, and over protected areas, he flies over as before.
- `TAIL` has five entries with a fifth value, the sideways offset. `kaiju_core` sweeps
  such a segment twice, once on either side, which gives the two splayed tails.
  Segments 2 to 5 are swept (8.4 to 20 units, i.e. 6.3 to 15 nodes behind him).
- `check_standin`: on the ground `kaiju_core.check_standin` with `SWIM_DEPTH = 12`; he
  wades through anything shallower at full speed. In flight the liquid type is dropped
  so that skimming water doesn't switch him to swim physics.
- `step_drowning` returns false. `gwp_penalties.WATER = 0`, so the pathfinder doesn't
  avoid water.

### 3.11 Animation lock (`kaiju_core.lock_animations`, `kaiju_core.play`)

On the ground mcl_mobs sets walk, stand and punch by itself every step. The one-shot
clips (beam, beam_fly, takeoff, land) set `_anim_lock` to their length, and the
`set_animation` override that `kaiju_core.lock_animations(ghidorah)` installs ignores
every request except "die" while it is above 0. A locked clip is started with
`kc.play(self, anim, time)`: it clears the lock and a punch animation that is still
timing out (which would turn the clip into "punch"), calls `set_animation`, then sets the
lock. The code is shared with Cthulhu ([`../kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md)
§3.12).

### 3.12 Saving and loading (`post_load_staticdata`)

mcl_mobs saves every non-function field. Bone overrides, the `physical` property and
the hit boxes are not saved, so on load:

- `_head_hp` is created if missing (this also initialises a fresh spawn);
- a flying Ghidorah is made non-physical again;
- `_beam_side` and the cached aim angles (`_aim_c/l/r`) are cleared, so the overrides
  are sent again;
- severed heads are scaled down again at once.

## 5. Model and texture pipeline

### 5.1 Conventions

The same as `tools/gojira_model.py` and `tools/mothra_model.py`
([`gojira/README.md`](../gojira/README.md) §5.1): Blender Z-up, the model faces +Y,
10 units = 1 node at `visual_size` 1, scene fps 1 so glTF seconds equal frame numbers,
rigid skinning (every box belongs to one bone), one 256×256 atlas.

`_l` is the +X side and `_r` the −X side. That is a label, not anatomy: the model
faces +Y, so **`_l` is his own right**. `head_l` (straight horns) is on his right,
`head_r` (curved horns, the outer one chipped) on his left. `init.lua` uses the same
labels (`model_point`, a wrapper around `kaiju_core.model_point` that takes model units:
side + = `_l`).

### 5.2 Skeleton (47 bones)

```
root
├─ hips
│  ├─ chest
│  │  ├─ neck_c1 → neck_c2 → neck_c3 → neck_c4 → head_c → jaw_c
│  │  ├─ neck_l1 → … → neck_l4 → head_l → jaw_l
│  │  ├─ neck_r1 → … → neck_r4 → head_r → jaw_r
│  │  ├─ wing_l_upper → wing_l_fore → wing_l_f1, wing_l_f2, wing_l_f3
│  │  └─ wing_r_upper → wing_r_fore → wing_r_f1, wing_r_f2, wing_r_f3
│  ├─ tail_a1 → … → tail_a5      (+X side)
│  └─ tail_b1 → … → tail_b5      (−X side)
├─ thigh_l → shin_l → foot_l
└─ thigh_r → shin_r → foot_r
```

- The legs hang from `root`, not `hips`, so a hip bob doesn't push the feet into the
  ground.
- `init.lua` uses the bone names `head_c/l/r` and `jaw_l/r` in bone overrides.
- `head_X` and `neck_X4` point exactly along +Y with roll 0, so their local axes are
  the model axes at rest. Luanti applies a relative override after the animation, about
  the parent's axes, so `neck_X4`'s frame decides what an aim rotation does.
- The side heads are 0.86 the size of the centre head (`HEAD_SCALE`).
- The end face of each `neck_X4` uses the red mouth tile: the stump of a severed head.
- The wings are modelled spread flat. Every animation poses them through `wings()`.

214 boxes, 1712 vertices before export, 4864 glTF vertices.

### 5.3 Animations

Frame ranges in `init.lua` (`animation = {...}`) must match the script.

| Clip | Frames | Speed (fps) | Loop | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `stand` | 0-60 | 15 | yes | 4 s | Idle. Each head has its own character: centre steady, `_l` slow sweeps, `_r` twitchy. Frame 0 is the neutral pose every one-shot clip starts and ends in. |
| `walk` | 70-110 | 5.3 × speed | yes | one stride cycle | Stance foot moves back at constant speed, root drops to keep it planted. |
| `fly` | 120-150 | 24 | yes | 1.25 s | Two wing beats, body leaning 52° |
| `hover` | 160-200 | 20 | yes | 2 s | Two slower beats, body 18° forward, necks weaving |
| `punch` | 210-230 | 25 | yes | 0.8 s | Bite: the heads lunge and snap one after the other (centre, `_l` 3 frames later, `_r` 6). The first snap is early because mcl_mobs deals the damage when the clip starts. |
| `beam` | 240-290 | 20 | no | 2.5 s | Rear back (246), aim forward with open jaws and spread wings, hold 250-284, close |
| `takeoff` | 300-320 | 20 | no | 1 s | Crouch, one big downbeat, ends in the hover pose |
| `land` | 330-350 | 20 | no | 1 s | From the hover pose: wings flare, legs reach down, crouch |
| `die` | 360-400 | 10 | no | 4 s | Knees buckle, he topples onto his +X side and lies on the ground |
| `beam_fly` | 410-450 | 20 | yes | 2 s | The hover beat with steady necks and open jaws |

The beams fire from 0.5 s to 2.5 s of a volley. In the "beam" clip the hold ends at
2.2 s, so the last tick falls into the closing movement.

### 5.4 Rotation conventions

- **Animation keys** (`key()`, armature space, degrees): X = pitch (+ lifts what lies
  ahead of the pivot), Y = roll (+ moves the +X side down), Z = yaw (+ swings the nose
  towards the −X side).
- **Bone overrides in Luanti**, measured in the client with `ghidorah_show` scenes
  12-14 (Luanti 5.17), for a relative rotation `v` on `head_X`: `v.x > 0` pitches the
  nose **down**, `v.y > 0` rolls the +X side down, `v.z > 0` turns the nose towards
  the **+X** side (clockwise seen from above). `aim_head` therefore sends
  `(-pitch, 0, -yaw_off)`.
- **Note:** the "head axes at rest" report printed by the script uses right-handed
  maths and shows the opposite signs for X and Z (+X = nose up, +Z = nose towards −X).
  The client measurement is what the code follows.

### 5.5 Texture atlas (`RECTS`, identical in both scripts)

| Tile | Rect (x, y, w, h) | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `SCALES` | 0, 0, 64, 64 | Body, necks, heads, tails |
| `SCALES_DARK` | 64, 0, 64, 64 | Thigh overlay, feet and soles, flanks, shoulders, brows, wing arms and fingers |
| `BELLY` | 128, 0, 64, 64 | Ribbed belly plates, throats, tail undersides |
| `CROWN` | 192, 0, 64, 64 | Dorsal, neck and tail spikes |
| `MEMBRANE` | 0, 64, 96, 64 | Wing membrane between fingers (alpha, scalloped edge) |
| `MEMBRANE_ARM` | 96, 64, 64, 64 | Membrane behind the arm bones (alpha) |
| `CRESCENT` | 160, 64, 64, 32 | Crescent plate on each forehead (alpha, mapped whole) |
| `FAN` | 160, 96, 64, 32 | Tail tip fans (alpha) |
| `EYE` | 224, 64, 32, 32 | Red eyes (mapped whole) |
| `MOUTH` | 224, 96, 32, 32 | Mouth interiors and neck stumps |
| `IVORY` | 0, 128, 64, 64 | Horns, teeth, fangs |
| `CLAW` | 64, 128, 64, 64 | Toe, spur, wrist and finger claws |
| `BLACK` | 128, 128, 32, 32 | Eye sockets, nostrils |

- Texel density is 4 px per model unit (`DENSITY`); each face gets a random offset
  inside its tile (`random.Random(42)` in the model, `Random(23)` in the texture, so
  both outputs are deterministic).
- Alpha tiles are fully transparent outside their outline. Luanti clips fully
  transparent pixels on entity meshes. The transparent filler has the membrane colour
  so that filtering at the edges doesn't bleed dark.
- Plates (membranes, fans, crescents) emit only their two big faces.
- Belly and membranes are paler than the scales, so body and wings read apart from a
  distance.

### 5.6 Self-checks and printed numbers

The model script asserts that the glTF has fewer than 65535 vertices, that the joints
equal the bone list and the parents are right, and that the lowest vertex of every
sampled walk frame is within 0.15 units of the ground. It prints:

| Print | Current value | Goes into |
|---|---|---|
| `stand frame 0: height … width …` | 26.92 units (20.2 nodes), width 41.98 units | §1 |
| `bind pose … wingspan` | 52.11 units (39.1 nodes) | §1 |
| `WALK_CYCLE_UNITS` | 10.057 | `WALK_CYCLE_NODES` |
| `<pose> head_X mouth … joint (…) pitch … yaw …` for stand f0, beam hold f260, fly f120, hover f160, beam_fly f410/f420 | | `HEAD_JOINT` (stand, reared, fly), `BEAM_POSE_PITCH` |
| `die frame …`, `die root lift` | lowest vertex 0.00 at frames 388 and 400 | check that he lies on the ground |
| `head axes at rest`, `blender check head_X` | | §5.4 |

The printed joints are `(forward, sideways, height)`, the order `HEAD_JOINT` uses. The
hover and beam_fly joints differ from the `reared` table by up to about 1.3 units
(1 node), which `init.lua` accepts.

The die pose is built in two passes: the script poses him with no lift, measures the
lowest vertex at frames 388 and 400, and raises `root` by that much.

### 5.7 Regenerating

From the repo root:

```sh
python3 tools/ghidorah_texture.py kaiju/ghidorah/textures/ghidorah.png
blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/ghidorah_model.py -- kaiju/ghidorah/models/ghidorah.glb
```

The texture needs Pillow. The model does not embed the image
(`export_image_format="NONE"`), so the order doesn't matter, but `RECTS` must be the
same in both scripts. After a model change, copy the printed numbers into
`init.lua:9-30` and check the frame ranges. Regenerating without changes reproduces
the committed `.glb` byte for byte.

## 7. Known limitations and gotchas

Status: **[code]** = verified by reading the code; **[test]** = observed in a test
run; **[unverified]** = plausible but not checked.

1. **The server doesn't know the animated pose** [code]. Head positions come from three
   fixed poses (§3.8). The idle sway, the walk bob and the lunge of the bite are not in
   them, and the hover clips are up to a node off. Beams and hit boxes are that far
   from where the client draws the heads.
2. **`_l` is his right** [code + test]. See §5.1. Mixing this up mirrors the hit boxes
   and beam origins.
3. **Override signs differ from the script's report** [test]. See §5.4. If head aim
   looks mirrored after an engine update, check `ghidorah_show` scenes 12-14 first.
4. **`set_bone_override` replaces the whole override of a bone** [code].
   `apply_head_scale` sends only a scale and so drops the aim rotation; `aim_head`
   always sends rotation and scale 1 (with 3 s interpolation, so it doesn't cut a
   growing head short). `aim_head` skips severed heads; `regrow_step` clears the angle
   cache (`_aim_X`) so a regrown head is turned to its target at once (fixed; before,
   it stayed unrotated until its aim changed by 0.03 rad).
5. **Aim adds some roll in the beam pose** [code]. The override turns about the parent
   neck's axes, which are tilted while he rears. A large yaw adds a few degrees of
   roll.
6. **A hit on a stump damages the body** [code + test]. The hit box of a severed head
   moves to the neck joint (`ghidorah_beam`: it stayed 2.25 nodes in front, where the
   skull was) and passes hits on as body damage.
7. **The beam stops at the first player or kaiju on the ray** [code + test]. That may
   not be his target. Smaller mobs are hurt and let it through.
8. **Beam damage on players** [code + test]. 1 per head and tick, 3 at most per tick,
   after a 0.5 s rear-up: a full volley takes 24 from a bare player. On VoxeLibre one
   head alone does nothing through iron armour and two nothing through diamond (armour
   rounds to whole HP). Cover works until the blasts remove it.
9. **Side-only volleys can be empty** [code + test]. They start whenever
   `_head_target_l` or `_head_target_r` is set, and the target can leave the cone
   before the first tick. (A severed head no longer has a target, so a stump alone
   starts none: fixed, `ghidorah_heads`.) He then plays the shoot sound,
   opens the side jaws, fires nothing and tries again after 2 s (`ghidorah_heads`,
   t = 18 and 36).
10. **Side heads only pick players and kaiju** [code], but they need an enemy other
    than the main target. With a single enemy in melee range no head beams.
11. **Stuck flights end the bulldoze (fixed)** [code + test]. The bulldoze timer only
    runs on the ground, so a take-off caused by a bulldoze used to find
    `_bulldoze_time` still set after landing and took off again, up to the cap of 3 per
    target. `take_off` now calls `kaiju_core.end_bulldoze` (`ghidorah_heads`:
    `stuck take-off: flying=true _bulldoze_time=nil`).
12. **Landing spots (fixed)** [code + test]. He used to land straight down, also on
    water, a roof or, without smashing, inside a tree. Now he looks for level, dry ground
    below him or around the target first (§3.4, `ghidorah_ceiling` phase "forest": he
    lands in a clearing instead of on the trees). Only five rays check a spot, so a
    single thin trunk between them is not seen; and when no spot is found he still comes
    down where he is.
13. **Roofs (fixed)** [code + test]. Flying he is non-physical, like Mothra. He no longer
    takes off through a roof (he needs 6 free nodes above his head, else he stays on
    foot), stays below a roof in flight, and lands when the way is blocked
    (`ghidorah_ceiling`; [`kaiju_core/README.md`](../kaiju_core/README.md) §3.6, §6.10).
    He looks for no way around: after a blocked flight he walks (and bulldozes) for 20 s
    before he tries again. Leaves and the caps of the Nether's huge fungi are no roof: he
    takes off through a canopy.
14. **Smashing in the air** [code + test]. Natural nodes stay, and a placed block of a
    natural kind (dirt, stone, plain sandstone) counts as natural: he flies over such a
    building. A tower loses the slice he flies through; the rest stays in the air.
    `ghidorah_airsmash`.
15. **Network cost** [code]. Up to 15 bone override messages a second, plus four hit
    box objects that move every server step. Fine for one Ghidorah.
16. **Staticdata keeps transient state** [code]. A volley, a cooldown, `_flying` and
    `_land` resume after a reload. ObjectRefs must stay in top-level fields
    (`_head_target_l`, `_hitbox_c`, ...): inside a table they would break saving.
17. **Rotating selection boxes don't work for server raycasts** [test, from the
    review]. A single box with `rotate = true` was tried instead of the hit box
    entities and dropped: in Luanti 5.17 server raycasts against it are wrong for any
    yaw other than 0.
18. **Target switching** [test]. In `ghidorah_fight` he kept the player as his target
    while Mothra attacked him, so the take-off for a flying enemy described in that
    scenario's header did not happen in the latest run. `ghidorah_heads` shows the
    take-off and landing.
19. **The damage flash tints him red for much of a fight** [test]. That is mcl_mobs'
    standard behaviour.

**Note:** the review notes list "creative players stop a beam" as open. The code has
it fixed: `is_enemy` asks `attack_player_allowed`, so beams pass through creative and
peaceful players.

## 8. Interactions

| Who | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ghidorah → Gojira, Mothra | Hunts both on sight (`hunts`, with `esp`), after retaliation and before players | `ghidorah/init.lua`, §3.3 |
| Gojira → Ghidorah | `build_nearest_target_rule("mob", {"ghidorah:ghidorah"}, nil, nil, true)` | `gojira/init.lua`, [`gojira/README.md`](../gojira/README.md) |
| Mothra → Ghidorah | `hunts_ghidorah` in her targeting rules | `mothra/init.lua`, [`mothra/README.md`](../mothra/README.md) |
| Ghidorah ↔ Cthulhu | He attacks Cthulhu within 32 nodes or when hit; Cthulhu attacks a walking Ghidorah in his territory | §3.3, [`cthulhu/README.md`](../cthulhu/README.md) §8 |

- **When he dies**, Gojira and Mothra part instead of fighting each other (§3.8,
  `kaiju_truce`).
- **Against Gojira** he walks and bites, and the two keep their bodies apart (§3.5):
  5.5 to 6.4 nodes between their centres, inside `BEAM_MIN_DIST` (8). In melee neither
  uses his breath or beams (Gojira's fire needs 9 nodes). A gust of Mothra's knocks
  Ghidorah 2 to 5 nodes away now and then; they are back in melee within a second, and
  only if the gap stays open for 1.5 s does Gojira get a volley (§3.5). Gojira stands
  below the centre head but does not reach it: his bites hit the body (§3.8).
- **Against Mothra** he takes off (she is airborne), flies level with her and beams.
  `MAX_ALTITUDE` keeps him from following her upwards without limit.
- **Against both** the side heads matter: while the body fights Gojira, they beam
  Mothra whenever she passes through their 60° cones (`ghidorah_heads`).
- **Balance.** The aim: one on one Gojira wins narrowly (10 to 35 % of his HP left);
  with Mothra's help Ghidorah dies and Gojira survives. Measured on flat ground with
  `kaiju_duel` and `kaiju_triangle` (winner, time of death, HP left):

  | | `kaiju_duel` (Gojira alone) | `kaiju_triangle` (Gojira + Mothra) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Before (melee standoff as first committed) | Ghidorah 5 of 5, at 105 s, with 235-246 of 600 left | Ghidorah 5 of 5: Gojira dead at 60-72 s, Ghidorah 161-197 left, Mothra 176-221 |
  | Bites hit the body, no hits on the ally | Gojira 5 of 5, at 101-102 s, with 12-18 of 500 left | Ghidorah dead 8 of 8 at 60-70 s, Gojira 17-203 left, Mothra 158-308 |
  | Plus the wait before a volley and bite 16 → 13 | Gojira 6 of 6, at 100-107 s, with 90-104 left (18-21 %) | Ghidorah dead 5 of 5 at 61-66 s, Gojira 173-283 left (35-57 %), Mothra 248-272 |
  | Plus the side heads' volleys at a melee target (16 per volley, every 12 s, no bite meanwhile) and bite 13 → 10 (now; Mineclonia) | **Gojira 6 of 6, at 96-102 s, with 80-112 left (16-22 %)** | **Ghidorah dead 4 of 4 at 57-61 s, Gojira 216-314 left, Mothra 197-254** |
  | The same on VoxeLibre | Gojira 6 of 6, at 98-100 s, with 84 left (17 %) | Ghidorah dead 4 of 4 at 56-60 s, Gojira 282-288 left, Mothra 236-278 |

  What was wrong, and what changed:

  1. *Bites into the centre head.* At the standoff Gojira stood under it, and 14 of
     every 34 bites were lost on a head that grows back. `HEAD_HIT_RADIUS` 3.5 → 2
     (§3.8).
  2. *Mothra hit her ally.* Gojira is a monster and stood next to her target, so her
     gusts and powder hit him as well and pushed him out of melee, into the beams: he
     died after 60 to 72 s instead of 105.
     She now spares him while her target is a Ghidorah
     ([`../mothra/README.md`](../mothra/README.md) §3.4).
  3. *Point-blank volleys.* Her gusts still knock Ghidorah himself back. Every time
     that took him past 8 nodes for an instant he fired a full volley (72 damage) at a
     Gojira who was already walking back in. `BEAM_MIN_TIME` (§3.5).
  4. With 1 to 3 the duel was Gojira's by 12 to 18 HP: in pure melee he deals 9 per
     bite to 600 HP and took 6.4 per bite on 500, after an opening exchange that costs
     him 72 and Ghidorah about 32. Ghidorah's bite went from 16 to 13 (5.2 per bite on
     Gojira) to widen that to about 95 HP. The beams, his danger to Mothra and to
     players, are untouched; so is his HP.

  5. *No beams in a one-on-one melee.* The side heads now fire at the enemy he is
     biting (§3.7, main side volley). In about 88 s of melee that is 7 volleys of 16,
     less the bites he skips meanwhile; his bite went from 13 to 10 to pay for it, which
     leaves the duel where it was (Gojira's HP left: 95 before, 80-112 after).

  The duel is nearly deterministic on flat ground, which is why the spread is so
  small; hills, water and buildings are not covered. In the triangle the spread comes
  from where Mothra's gusts push him. Further knobs if it needs retuning: his bite
  `damage`, `BEAM_MIN_TIME`, `HEAD_HIT_RADIUS`, his `hp_max`.
- Other Ghidorahs are never enemies: not for retaliation, not for the beams.
